# SPACE FLEET

A two-player (or one against the bot) fleet battle for a Quest headset in its browser. This repo is the whole game and its server; it was split out of `thestrangestfleamarket-collab/thestrangest` on 19 Nov 2026 (owner: "i want to separate the repos from each other, the market doesnt need the space code") and shares nothing with it.

The owner (Bralynn) decides how it looks and plays. His decisions, with his words and dates, are in `docs/HISTORY.md`: look there before changing how something looks or behaves, and add a lean dated line for each new decision. Commit messages, pull requests and replies stay short.

## Layout

`src/space/` is SPACE FLEET, a fleet battle for a Quest headset in its browser on its own page (`/space`, to anyone with the link): the field floats in your room in passthrough, or in open space (the VIEW button, A or X); two players, or one against the bot, and you pick your navy on the page (ADMIRALTY or CORSAIRS, each its own ships and bent: `NAVY` in `sim.js`). `sim.js` is the whole battle with no screen or network, ten steps a second (ships fly with inertia and fight on their own: shot at they answer, standing by they go to a friend under fire close by; shots take time to arrive and can miss, small fast ships dodging most; orders, which queue into a mission (`give`, `advance`) and are carried out as ATTACK, DEFEND or SNEAK (`stance`: a sneaking ship is unseen from afar until it fires or is hit), the shipyard, relays for income, a big fleet's upkeep, mending beside your own mothership, force fields, blasts that shove; the win is the other mothership); the first player in is the host and runs it, the other follows its pictures (`snap`/`read`) and sends orders; everyone at `/space` is in its one public room, the `Space` Durable Object in `worker/door.js` (two players, the battle starts when the second joins; what may pass in `worker/space.js`); if a player leaves, the bot takes their fleet. `bot.js` is the bot: its admiral is Claude Haiku (`worker/space.js` `think`, POST `/space/mind`, for anyone who plays, the `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` secret, a daily count each), its own instinct otherwise. `view.js` draws it (real models, one draw a part; three hulls for each kind of ship in each navy, `<kind>-<navy>-<n>.glb`, the second and third loaded behind the start; every hull painted its fleet's colour, light blue or pink, over its own light and dark (`paint`); each navy has its own build (`shape`: the Admiralty's longer and narrower with a pointed bow and a tail fin, the Corsairs' broad with side pods and short horns, every added piece sunk into the hull); every hull carries detail with depth: `greeble` stands plates, blocks, pipes, hatches and masts on it as geometry at load, and `detail` lays a plating relief over it (`data/space/tex/`, drawn by `python3 tools/space-detail.py`: strakes, plates, fitted panels, bays, vents, hatches, conduits; the Corsairs' patched and dented), each pixel traced into its height map, in two sizes, fading out when small on screen; a blown ship comes apart into pieces of its own hull that move by `step()`'s physics with the rocks: impulses, momentum, spin, collisions, nothing played back; fire is simulated gas, `Fire`, each puff a glowing ball in the field's space, never a picture turned to face you), `cosmos.js` open space built of bodies in three dimensions (every star a glowing ball far off, drawn as the meteors are: the Yale Bright Star Catalogue's stars from `data/stars.json`, a galaxy, far clusters and clouds of gas, a sun; no sky picture, no painted points, no planet), `post.js` the lens for open space only (depth of field and motion blur; the console, notices and pointers are on layer 3, drawn after it; off unless LENS on the console turns it on), `main.js` the headset and hands (trigger a ship and drag it to order it, again to add to its mission, two taps to cancel it; a label at each pointer says what the trigger will do; every ship of yours shows its mission as a line, gone when flown or changed; a bubble to pick many, B or Y for all, then a trigger pull in open space sends the picked ships there; the right thumbstick zooms and turns the field, the left flies, a click pauses for both players; grips move, turn and size the field), `hud.js` the console (`Panel`: one board floating at your left that either controller points at and presses: the shipyard, pause, the view, the guide, the lens; a grip on it carries it) and the guide (`Guide`: a board that shows one step at a time at a player's first battle, the bot waiting meanwhile). Its models are Sketchfab's through Objaverse (`PWCHROME=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium node tools/space-models.cjs`), its sounds recordings (`python3 tools/space-sounds.py`); credits in `docs/space-credits.md` and `docs/space-sounds.md`. `tools/space.cjs` bundles it into `dist/space.html` and copies `data/space/` to `dist/space-assets/`; `tests/fleet.cjs` checks the rules, the admiral, two linked pages and an emulated Quest 3; `node tools/space-play.cjs [games]` plays it as a player against the bot (each move, what it should do, what it did; then whole battles).

`worker/index.js` is the server (Cloudflare Worker, `wrangler.jsonc`): the page at `/` and `/space`, `/space-assets/`, the room (`Space`, partyserver), the admiral's route with a daily count per player (`Tally`). The `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` secret is set on the worker; without it the bot plays by its own instinct.

Every file in `src/` starts with one comment line saying what it is; otherwise comments are rare and short.

## Build, check, run

- `npm install` once; `npm run build` writes `dist/space.html` and `dist/space-assets/`.
- `PWCHROME=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium npm test` runs `tests/fleet.cjs` (the rules, the admiral, two linked pages, an emulated Quest 3; slow under software rendering).
- `npm run dev` serves it locally (`wrangler dev`); merging to `main` is going live once Cloudflare builds this repo (`npm run build`, then deploy).
- The page takes `?sim` (no headset), `?fast=n`, `?navy=0|1`, `?fx`, `?guide`, `?local&slot=n` for tests.

## The rules

- Never break play: no frozen page; the lens off unless asked; nothing that glows may draw black in passthrough.
- Nothing that changes how the game looks or plays is added, changed or undone without the owner. A request that could mean more than one thing gets options or a mockup first.
- A change ships with the check that proves it; done means it builds, `tests/fleet.cjs` passes and the owner has approved it.
