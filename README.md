# thestrangest (The Strangest Flea Market in three.js)

The Strangest Flea Market, rebuilt in three.js, a piece at a time. Separate from the live game (`bralynnb/tsfm`), which it reads only as data.

Build: `python3 build.py` (needs `../tsfm`, a checkout of the game, or `GAME_SRC=/path/to/tsfm/src`) → `dist/market.html` (**THE MARKET**, the one official map: https://claude.ai/artifact/X2Bk8UjQKP8yfLdLtVVn3s).

## Now

One street at night: the bodega (its shelves, coolers, goods, counter, back office, lights) and the pool room (real water, swimming, floats)
side by side; the subway station underneath, twelve steps down from the sidewalk, the goldfish and the bear on its platform. The 3D Walker
with his moves, ragdoll and physics; real lights and shadows; free rotation and zoom, pixels that grow with the zoom.

## Not here yet (what the game has)

Places: the rest of the map (the block, the West End, Central Park, the zoo, the harbor, the theatre, the bar, the apartments, the museum,
the themed rooms, the VR places). Characters: the other NPCs, animals and pets, the drones. Things: the object catalog, street dressing, room
lights with switches, fish tanks. Activities: computers, TVs, radios, instruments, VR, riding, boxing, sitting, the bag, item cards, Day 1.
World: time of day, weather, wind, sound. Online: multiplayer, accounts, talking, ASL. Menus: settings, map, editors, gamepad, route finding.
