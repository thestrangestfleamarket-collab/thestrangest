## What it needs to work

- **The city back on.** You turned off jobs, schedules and the city's services on 19 Oct (`CITY.on`, `?city=1`). This plan needs `JOBS` and the daily schedules on again. Its work list still names places that are gone (the diner, the aquarium, the Lucky, the Yellow, the baker, the cottage); it gets rebuilt from the list above.
- **One file per character, loaded near.** Each body is about 4,000 triangles and 70 KB; all 193 would be about 13 MB and 770,000 triangles. Each character's data loads only when you are near its place, and is drawn only near and in view.
- **Many players.** Schedules run off the shared clock, so every player sees the same person at the same place at the same hour; nothing is saved per player.
- **Checks.** A check that walks the clock through a day and proves each worker reaches their job and gets home.

## Decided (owner, 2 Nov 2026)

1. Jobs and hours back on (walk to work, go home at night); the city's services (fires, patrols, tills, wages) stay off.
2. The themed 44 come in now as visitors: they walk Section A by day with no job and leave by the subway at night.
3. The 18 townspeople live in the apartment house: in at the front door at night, out in the morning. They never take a player's apartment and have no inside.
4. The diner gets built on an empty lot, with its 5 jobs.

## Built (2 Nov 2026, steps 1 to 5)

All 192 are in the game; the Kid in a Cardboard Robot Costume is not published on polyfork yet. Each character's `job` is in `data/characters.json`, editable under JOB on the character editor's behaviour tab. Bodies load one place at a time as you come within reach of it. The Loop's staff work below and go down the subway stairs. The night market's sellers keep night stalls at the flea market (the night market has no way in yet). Indoor staff stand inside the in-place shops (bar, bank, both museums, theatre) and the off-map ones (diner, arcade, playhouse, bodega, boardwright, fix-it); at the rest (the civic row, the bath house, the feel lab, the castle) they are out of sight while at work.
