# SPACE FLEET: the owner's decisions

Carried over from thestrangestfleamarket-collab/thestrangest's docs/HISTORY.md on 19 Nov 2026 (the entries that name SPACE FLEET). Add a dated line for each new decision; keep them lean.

- 16 Nov 2026: SPACE FLEET at `/space` (owner: "a 2 player real time vr browser based strategy game like red alert"; "augmented reality where you control scifi style ships"; "quest headsets only", "you can see only the ships and the rest of your room, or switch to open space"; "you can grab the ships, move them ... but they act alone (like red alert) you can give them orders"; "2 player or 1 player via bot - game starts as soon as both players join - host at /space"; "real available space 3d ship models, textures", "sfx, sounds, movements, physics, lights, shadows"; "if they explode it should be real explosion physics, pieces fly apart, no preprogrammed animations"; "warp effects, forcefield, use haiku for full AI control of ships minds"; "you can pick which side of the space war you are on, each side gets different ships"; "no flat images" for the sky). Rules: it shares no code with THE MARKET; nothing of a blast is played back; the sky is built, not pictured. Not done: depth of field and motion blur (whole-picture passes in a headset; to be decided).
- 16 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's sky (owner: "no planet, faroff distance stars and formations, small realistic meteors nearby"; "stars way too crisp and pixelated sharp"): no planet or moon; far clusters, a galaxy and gas clouds built of points; the rocks are small meteors; every star a soft point.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET (owner: "add depth of field and motion blur in open space view, remove the stars background, use the same method you're doing for the meteors but make them far away and bright like stars but in real 3d space, no flat images. add a guide to show you how to play at the start, its too confusing, allow to zoom in and out closer to ships and to pause the battle and move around in the 3d space"; "once you assign a ship a goal it follows its mission, you can give it multiple things to do"): a lens in open space only; stars are bodies, not points; a guide at the first battle; thumbsticks zoom, turn and fly; a pause for both players; orders queue into a mission. Rules: the lens never touches the room view or the wrist panel; an order adds to a mission, two taps cancel it.
- 17 Nov 2026, SWIVEL at `/turret` (owner: "create a VR browser game where you are in a ship turrent and can spin around flying and shooting at other ships. (ideal for sitting in spinning office chair)"). Mine, for him to change: the name, the ship flying itself, endless waves with a cruiser every fifth, a screen as well as a headset, SPACE FLEET's models and sounds. Not in the holodeck's list yet.
- 17 Nov 2026, SWIVEL checked against the standing list (owner: "Look at aesthetic complaints and instructions I give you often and apply them all"): no sky picture, stars as bodies and the lens as in SPACE FLEET; dark, lit by the sun, its display and its guns; real fittings and worn metal from HULL DOWN; no flicker, less glow; wreckage is the ship's own hull; 480 lines; a guide at the first fight. Rule: SWIVEL follows the list in 16 Nov's THE LAST CHANCE entry.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's console (owner: "i cant figure out how to use the left control, nothing i do selects the ships on my panel, redo the controls, menus more intuitive"): the wrist panel is gone; one big console floats at your left, either controller presses it, a grip carries it. Rule: every menu button works with either hand.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's lens off by default (owner, of open space in the headset: "it looked all blurry doesnt look right at all"): open space is drawn plain and sharp; the lens is only on when LENS on the console is pressed, and is gentler.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's fleets (owner, of the ships shown from five free sources: "lets add them all but recolor them to either light blue or light green tones overall (with shades, wear tear etc, realistic color shades)"; his pick of how many: "Best 36"): three hulls for each kind of ship in each navy; one fleet light blue, the other light green.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's fights last longer (owner: "the ships are dying too fast, the ship battles should take longer because of more misses, delayed shots, shield, dodging, etc."): shots fly and can miss, small fast ships dodge, hulls and fields are stronger.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's orders (owner: "when you send your ships to locations you should be able to give them orders like defend, attack, sneak, etc. make more involved strategy as leader"): ATTACK, DEFEND, SNEAK and FALL BACK on the console; sneaking ships are unseen from afar.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET: CONTROL, and fire with depth (owner: "lets add the game as an ability in /space if you click \"control\" as option on ship you can fly it yourself and battle in game"; "when i zoomed in the explosions looked like flat images"): pick one of your ships and press CONTROL on the console to fly it yourself from its back; a grip hands it back. Fire is balls of light in the field, not pictures. Rules: one hand-flown ship a side; the field never turns with the ship (you turn); a hand-flown ship reaches half as far again and turns quicker than it does alone. Mine, for him to change: you ride the ship's own back, not SWIVEL's turret; no change to its hull or damage.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's light (owner: "lighting looks washed out, ship too bright, add realistic lighting"): one hard sun, the side away from it near black, little fill, dimmer reflections, lower exposure; a blast's flash falls off with distance instead of whitening every hull near it. Rule: no hull is lit past white.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's hulls with depth (owner: "look for realistic spaceship textures, panels, etc to use to give the ships more detail. dont paint flat images on, give real depth to everything on ships and in game. need to look extremely details and intricate when zoomed in to the pixel"): real panel sets (ambientCG, CC0) laid over every hull as relief you see into, in two sizes, and plates, blocks, pipes, hatches and masts standing on the hulls as geometry. Rule: detail on a ship has depth (geometry, or a height map traced per pixel), never a flat picture.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's ships and hull relief replaced (owner: "find better ship models and hull panel sets"; of the candidates, "your picks"): nine new first hulls (the Corsair mothership, both cruisers, frigates and gun platforms, the Admiralty fighter, the Corsair mine; the Admiralty's Harbinger, already one of its three motherships, kept at more detail), all CC BY from Sketchfab through Objaverse, kept at more triangles than before; the Corsair fighter and the Admiralty mine stay. The hull relief is drawn here: the free libraries' sci-fi sets were floor tiles, and the good ones (freepbr) are not free to ship. SWIVEL takes the same ships; its drones are the horned mine. Rule: a model is only taken with a licence that lets it ship (CC0 or CC BY), credited in `docs/space-credits.md`.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET: no flying a ship yourself (owner: "remove the ability to control the ships in 1st person / turret/swivel etc"): CONTROL is gone from the console; you command, you do not pilot. Rule: SPACE FLEET has no first-person or turret view of a ship. (SWIVEL, the separate turret game at /turret, is not touched.)
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's order lines (owner: "im seeing black lines from where the ships went, the lines should vanish after they arrive where they went or path changes"): each of your ships shows its mission as a line (green to a place, red to a target) that goes when it arrives, is stopped or is given another. Rule: no line outlives its order; nothing that glows may draw black in passthrough.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET: every ship its job, every move said (owner: "make sure each ship has a reason to exist - dont get too complicate"; "make sure its intuitive"): the console names each ship's job, what it beats and what kills it (fighter RAIDER, frigate ESCORT, cruiser SIEGE, turret GUARD, mine TRAP); a label at the pointer says what the trigger will do. Corsairs: thin fields, harder-hitting guns.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's hulls less washed out (owner: "increase texture quality of ships - they look too washed out, make realistic texture , lighting, details"): twenty hulls' textures at twice the size; the fleet colour laid over a hull keeps more of its own light and dark. The light itself is as set earlier today.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET played through (owner: "simulate games against you playing vs the bot and then look for issues ... fine tune"): the bot now raids relays with two sneaking fighters, gathers before it goes, puts all guns on one ship and falls back when beaten.
- 17 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET after five watched battles (owner, of the five faults they showed: "Fix all"): a force field is one shell however many shots strike it (no white blobs); the bot opens three ways and on either flank; a losing side can come back (less income from relays, a big fleet costs to run, ships are mended beside their own mothership); motherships stand longer; the bot saves for cruisers and never flies mostly fighters.
- 18 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET: ships with their own sense, and sending a group (owner: "if something flies close up to attack they should fight back, if they see their friends need help nearby they help out"; "need to be easier to select all ships and send them to a location"): a ship shot at answers, even on DEFEND (no farther than its post allows); a ship standing by goes to a friend under fire close by, to its mothership from farther. With ships picked, one trigger pull in open space sends them all there; B or Y picks all, again lets them go.
- 18 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's cruisers and turrets brighter (owner: "make the cruisers and turrets brighter so theyre easier to see"): their paint lighter and less mirror-like; the light itself unchanged.
- 19 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET's fleets are light blue and pink (owner: "do more paint jobs, but make them light blue vs pink"; of six shown, "1"): the tinted paint as it was, the second fleet pink where it was green.
- 19 Nov 2026, SPACE FLEET: each navy its own build (owner: "lets make the ships more distinct shapes on each team, they look too similiar now"; "not so long sharp, make sure all part of shsips are connected no floating parts"): the Admiralty's ships longer and narrower with a pointed bow and a tail fin, the Corsairs' broad with side pods and short horns. Rule: nothing added to a hull floats free of it, and nothing long and needle-sharp.
