# Skirmish Table: credits

- People (`data/chess/units/`, baked by `tools/chess-bake.cjs` from the originals in `data/full/chess/characters/`): Quaternius (quaternius.com), CC0: Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy (the free Standard tier: ranger and peasant outfits), Universal Base Characters (heads, hair); each unit type joined into one mesh, thinned to about two and a half thousand triangles, on one shared 1024 by 1024 picture. Their weapons, shields, hats, capes, and the collar and belt in their side's colour, are made in the bake.
- Motion (`data/chess/units/anim.glb`): Quaternius, Universal Animation Library and Universal Animation Library 2 (the free Standard files), CC0: eighteen clips kept (`tools/chess-creatures.py` names them), the fingers left out, fifteen keys a second.
- Creatures (22 Nov 2026, remade the same day to match the people), all CC0:
  - Ogre, Ghost: the people's own body made over in the bake (the Ogre a third larger and heavier, its skin tinted, with a club, tusks, a fur mantle and a hide; the Ghost the hooded ranger's top over a ragged robe, no head and no legs, seen through). Built here, not found models.
  - Skeleton: built in the bake from simple shapes (bones, ribs, a skull), each carried by one of the people's bones. Built here, not a found model.
  - Wolf: Quaternius, Ultimate Animated Animals (quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html), `Wolf.gltf`, with its own clips (Idle, Gallop, Attack, Idle_HitReact1, Death); its tail turned down in the bake.
  - Dragon Whelp: "Cethiel's Dragon 3D" by kooow (Drummyfish), after Cethiel's 2D dragon, CC0, from OpenGameArt (opengameart.org/content/cethiels-dragon-3d): its model, picture and four clips (idle, walk, attack, die); in the bake its tail is curled round, its walk's leg swings made three times wider, its picture brightened, and a short struck clip made (it came with none).
  - Their surfaces (`data/full/chess/creatures/tex/`, painted into the shared picture with their relief): Poly Haven (polyhaven.com), CC0: rock_boulder_cracked (the club's head), white_rough_plaster (bone), leather_white (hide), rough_linen (the robe, rags), polar_fleece (fur).
  - The Quaternius files were fetched from an unchanged copy of the packs (the `series-ai/jam-ready-assets` repository on GitHub, which names the same source and licence), because the packs' own Google Drive folders refuse downloads.
- The pieces' own sounds (22 Nov 2026: footfalls by body, weapons, voices, the room), each a recording, are listed one by one in `docs/chess-sounds.md`.
- Sprites (`data/chess/fx/`) and the first sounds (`data/chess/sfx/`): Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0: Particle Pack, Impact Sounds, RPG Audio, Sci-Fi Sounds, Interface Sounds; sounds as mono MP3, sprites at 256 px.
- The dungeon and the mountainside round it (`data/chess/dungeon/`, fetched by `tools/chess-dungeon.py` into `data/full/chess/dungeon/`, baked by `tools/chess-bake.cjs trees` and `tools/chess-bake.cjs dungeon`), all CC0. Poly Haven (polyhaven.com): surfaces medieval_blocks_03 (the board's flagstones), castle_brick_broken_06 (the brick wall and towers), mossy_stone_wall (the low walls, piers and fallen stones), rock_wall_08 (the moat's sides), mossy_rock (the bank), medieval_blocks_05, and for the ground's painted picture leafy_grass, forrest_ground_01, cliff_side, rocks_ground_05, aerial_rocks_02; models namaqualand_boulder_02, rock_moss_set_01, stone_fire_pit, large_iron_gate, treasure_chest, wine_barrel_01, wooden_crate_01, kite_shield, each thinned; the sky, kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky (its tonemapped picture, made quieter); the trees, fir_sapling, pine_sapling_small and island_tree_02, each whole tree pictured from the side for the cards and the far picture (a grown fir or pine is tiers of the sapling's pictures; the whole trees, 80 MB, are not kept). ambientCG (ambientcg.com): Lava001 (the lava, its emission picture), LeafSet029 (the ivy's leaves). The land's shape, the hall, the beacon, the chains, the water, mist, birds and drifting cloud are made in the bake or the page.
- Everything else (how the dungeon is laid out and lit, the labels, weapons, shields, capes, banners and flasks, reverb, footsteps and whooshes) is made in the page or its bake. The later types' effects and sounds reuse the Kenney sprites and sounds above; no file was added for them.

Set aside in `archive/chess-units/` (22 Nov 2026), not on the website: eight people (Pikeman, Shieldbearer, Crossbowman, Assassin, Bard, Frost Mage, Summoner, Storm Mage), the same Quaternius bodies with gear made at the bake, and the Golem, a stand-in: Quaternius' Ultimate Monsters `Big/Yeti.gltf` (CC0) with every colour turned to stone at the bake.
