# Skirmish Table: credits

- People (`data/chess/units/`, baked by `tools/chess-bake.cjs`; realistic since 23 Nov 2026): the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library (github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox), MIT (`docs/licences/rocketbox-mit.txt`), fetched by `tools/chess-people.py` into `data/full/chess/people/`. Which body is which: Knight `Professions/Police_Female_01`, Berserker `Professions/Delivery_Male_01`, Rogue `Adults/Male_Adult_18`, Archer `Adults/Female_Adult_04`, Alchemist `Professions/Gardener_Male_01`, Fire Mage `Adults/Male_Adult_19`, Cleric `Adults/Female_Adult_06`, Enchanter `Adults/Female_Adult_11`, Necromancer `Adults/Female_Adult_10`, Captain `Professions/Fire_Male_06`, Ogre `Professions/Sports_Male_01`, Ghost `Adults/Female_Adult_16`. Faces, hands and hair are theirs as photographed; their clothes are painted over in the bake (the photograph's folds kept, the colour and weave replaced) and covered by what is made there on each body's own shape: tabards, stoles, sashes, belts, cowls, cloaks, an apron, pauldrons. Each is thinned to about 2,000 to 2,700 triangles on one shared picture of 2048 by 2816.
- Motion (`data/chess/units/anim.glb`): walking, running and standing are Rocketbox's own (`m_walk_neutral_01`, `m_run_neutral_01`, `m_idle_neutral_01`, `f_walk_neutral_01`, `f_idle_neutral_01`; MIT), put onto the clips' bones in the bake. Striking, casting, shooting, being struck and falling are Quaternius' Universal Animation Library and Universal Animation Library 2 (the free Standard files), CC0: eighteen clips kept (`tools/chess-creatures.py` names them), the fingers left out, fifteen keys a second. Rocketbox has no fighting motion.
- Creatures:
  - Ogre: Rocketbox's `Sports_Male_01` made a third larger, heavier, heavy-jawed and green in the bake, with tusks, a hide, a fur and spiked iron on its shoulders made there. A body found, remade here.
  - Ghost: Rocketbox's `Female_Adult_16`, a figure draped head to foot, made pale and seen through. Found.
  - Skeleton: built in the bake (long bones, ribs, hips, a skull), each piece carried by one of the people's bones. Built here: no CC0 or MIT anatomical skeleton was found (the CC0 skull scans looked at were a broken brain case and a carved netsuke).
  - Wolf: Rocketbox's `Animals/Dog_GermanShepard_01` (MIT), its coat turned grey in the bake. It came with no motion: it is moved by the clips of Quaternius' wolf (Ultimate Animated Animals, CC0, `data/full/chess/creatures/Wolf.gltf`: Idle, Gallop, Attack, Idle_HitReact1, Death), each bone turned as the wolf's is.
  - Dragon Whelp: "Green iguana" (Iguana iguana), a scan of a mounted animal by the State Darwin Museum (darwinmuseum.ru), CC0, Sketchfab model 217da036aae14762904ef4630023978b, fetched from Objaverse's copy by `tools/chess-whelp.cjs`, which takes it off its board and the log under its forefeet, thins it and bakes its skin from the scan's own photographs. Its bones, its two wings and its five clips are made in the bake: no realistic rigged dragon under CC0 or MIT was found.
- What is held: the sword (also the Rogue's dagger and the Skeleton's blade), the kite shield and the axe are Poly Haven's scanned models (polyhaven.com), CC0: antique_estoc, kite_shield, wooden_axe, each thinned. Staffs, the crossbow, flasks, the club, the banner, the quiver, the pack and the book are made in the bake.
- Surfaces (`data/full/chess/creatures/tex/`, painted into the shared picture with their relief): Poly Haven, CC0: metal_plate_02 (steel), brown_leather, poly_wool_herringbone (wool, and the cloth in each side's colour), rough_linen, hessian_230, wood_table_worn, white_rough_plaster (bone), leather_white (hide, the whelp's wings), polar_fleece (fur); ambientCG (ambientcg.com), CC0: Chainmail004 (mail).
- The pieces' own sounds (22 Nov 2026: footfalls by body, weapons, voices, the room), each a recording, are listed one by one in `docs/chess-sounds.md`.
- Sprites (`data/chess/fx/`) and the first sounds (`data/chess/sfx/`): Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0: Particle Pack, Impact Sounds, RPG Audio, Sci-Fi Sounds, Interface Sounds; sounds as mono MP3, sprites at 256 px.
- The dungeon and the mountainside round it (`data/chess/dungeon/`, fetched by `tools/chess-dungeon.py` into `data/full/chess/dungeon/`, baked by `tools/chess-bake.cjs trees` and `tools/chess-bake.cjs dungeon`), all CC0. Poly Haven (polyhaven.com): surfaces medieval_blocks_03 (the board's flagstones), castle_wall_slates (the hall's walls and towers, 23 Nov 2026) and stone_wall_05 (their dressed stone: buttresses, the gate's arch, battlements' tops), each with its relief and hollows painted in from its normal and occlusion maps, mossy_stone_wall (the fallen stones), rock_wall_08 (the moat's sides), mossy_rock (the bank), medieval_blocks_05, and for the ground's painted picture leafy_grass, forrest_ground_01, cliff_side, rocks_ground_05, aerial_rocks_02; models namaqualand_boulder_02, rock_moss_set_01, stone_fire_pit, large_iron_gate, treasure_chest, wine_barrel_01, wooden_crate_01, kite_shield, each thinned; the sky, kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky (its tonemapped picture, made quieter); the trees, fir_sapling, pine_sapling_small and island_tree_02, each whole tree pictured from the side for the cards and the far picture (a grown fir or pine is tiers of the sapling's pictures; the whole trees, 80 MB, are not kept). ambientCG (ambientcg.com): Lava001 (the lava, its emission picture), LeafSet029 (the ivy's leaves). The land's shape, the hall's walls, towers and gatehouse (`tools/chess-hall.page.js`; their light a baked picture), the beacon, the chains, the water, mist, birds and drifting cloud are made in the bake or the page.
- Everything else (how the dungeon is laid out and lit, the labels, reverb, footsteps and whooshes) is made in the page or its bake. The later types' effects and sounds reuse the Kenney sprites and sounds above; no file was added for them.
