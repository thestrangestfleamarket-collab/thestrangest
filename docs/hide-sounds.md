# Hide (VR alley hide-and-seek) — sound credits

All files in `data/hide/snd/` are real recordings, converted with ffmpeg to MP3 96 kbps CBR, 44.1 kHz,
mono (rain is stereo), peak-normalised to about -3 dBFS, metadata stripped. Total about 1.0 MB.

Sources:

- **BigSoundBank** (Joseph Sardin, https://bigsoundbank.com) — every sound page states
  "License CC0 (public domain): Free and royalty-free"; site licence page: https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html.
  No attribution required (credit is appreciated).
- **OpenGameArt** — "10 Impact/Shield Blocks" by StarNinjas, licence shown on the page: CC0.

"Loop" = the tail is cross-faded into the head (equal power) so the file loops seamlessly, plus 8 ms
micro-fades at both ends so decoders that keep MP3 padding do not click.
One-shots have a 4-6 ms fade-in and the fade-out noted.

## Notes

- The footsteps (step1-4) were recorded on **dry** concrete with ordinary shoes, not boots on a wet surface;
  layer `splash.mp3` or `drip.mp3` quietly under them for a wet feel.
- `metal1`/`metal2`/`lid` are a household-size metal trash can, not a dumpster; pitch them down in the
  game (playbackRate 0.6-0.8) for a bigger container.
- `glass.mp3` is a clink only, there is no rolling part.
- `rat.mp3` is a mouse, not a rat; play slightly slower for a bigger animal.
- Clips were chosen by waveform/level analysis, not by ear — worth one listening pass.
