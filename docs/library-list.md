# The bookstore's books

16 bookcases, one genre each, 4 shelves × 10 books = 640 real books, shelved by author surname then title. Every one is US public domain (first published 1930 or earlier; translations from before 1931) and is an English text on Project Gutenberg (PG number in brackets). The data is `data/library.json`.

## Notes

**Left out (not public domain in the US, or not on Gutenberg in a pre-1931 English text)**: Kafka's *Metamorphosis* and Hesse's *Siddhartha* (Gutenberg has only modern translations); Zola's *Nana*, Woolf's *To the Lighthouse*, Faulkner's *As I Lay Dying*, *Red Harvest*, *Beau Geste* and Forster's *The Machine Stops* (not on Gutenberg); *Right Ho, Jeeves*, *At the Mountains of Madness* and Seven Pillars of Wisdom (published after 1930).

**Moved between cases**: *Northanger Abbey* is with Horror & Gothic (it sends up the gothic novel), *Walden* and *The Voyage of the Beagle* are with Science & Nature, *Moby-Dick* is with American Classics, *Heart of Darkness* is with British Classics, *The Canterville Ghost* is with Humour, *The Golden Bough* and *The Wealth of Nations* are with Philosophy & Religion.

**Only one volume exists on Gutenberg, so volume 1 is used**: *Le Morte d'Arthur*, Gibbon's *Decline and Fall* and Herodotus' *Histories*. *The Tale of Genji* is Waley's 1925 first part.

**Editions that hold more than their title**: *The Cherry Orchard* is in Chekhov's *Plays, Second Series*; *Six Characters in Search of an Author* is in Pirandello's *Three Plays*; *Miss Julie* was dropped. *Oedipus the King* is Storr's three Theban plays, *Everyman* is Rhys's *Everyman, with other interludes*, *The Monkey's Paw* is part 2 of *The Lady of the Barge*, *The Awakening* comes with selected stories and *Walden* with *Civil Disobedience*. The edition titles for *The Captain's Daughter* (*The Daughter of the Commandant*), *Père Goriot* (*Father Goriot*), *The Hunchback of Notre-Dame* (*Notre-Dame de Paris*), *Dangerous Liaisons* (*Dangerous Connections*) and *The Wanderer* (*Le Grand Meaulnes*) are different. Boswell's *Life of Johnson* is Osgood's one-volume abridgement.

**Doubts**: The catalogue names no translator for *Manon Lescaut* (PG 468), *L'Assommoir* (PG 8558), *Candide* (PG 19942), *The Betrothed* (PG 35155) or *The Nicomachean Ethics* (PG 8438). They look like 19th-century or early-1900s translations, but nobody has checked. *Emil and the Detectives* is the 1930 American edition. Dates for ancient and medieval works are approximate. Page counts are estimates. Spine colours are plausible cloth colours, not taken from real copies. Some books have dated racial content the owner may want to review: *Doctor Dolittle*, *Fu-Manchu* and *Uncle Tom's Cabin*.
