# Walkabout Mini Golf: how to play, controls and settings

Companion to `walkabout-mini-golf.md`. Verified 2 Nov 2026 against full page reads of Mighty Coconut's official resources page, glossary and support knowledge base (articles dated Feb to Oct 2026, including the official Quest 2, Quest 3 and PS VR2 controller diagrams), the Steam FAQ thread written by Mighty Coconut staff (Jan 2022), two Steam community guides, VR Lowdown, Maecker VR, The Lady M, Gaming Age and UploadVR. Items marked *(community)* come only from player guides; *(unverified)* means the page sits behind a bot check and only a search snippet was seen. Everything else is official.

## 1. The one-controller model

- The whole game is played with **one controller**. Pick either hand; the game puts you on the matching side of the ball. "The entire game can be played with nothing but that trigger button if you are on default settings" (Steam FAQ).
- Put the unused controller outside your boundary or out of the room, don't wake it, or pull its battery. It can block the headset's view of the live one. On PS VR2, hold its PS button 10 seconds to switch it off.
- To change hands, pick up the other controller and bring it into your boundary. If you putt with the "wrong" controller (lefty on the right one or vice versa), turn on **Invert Hands** (Putter tab) so the trigger aligns you to the correct side.
- Hold the controller with your dominant hand and steady your wrist with the other hand, like a real putter. Don't grab the tracking ring with the free hand, hold the putter so low your body hides it, or play under fairy lights or by mirrors and windows at night: the putter head will glitch.
- Seated play works but the boundary must be on so the game knows the floor. Turn on **Lock Ball Position**, press trigger once to align, move your chair, and from then on the trigger places the ball in the same spot every time.

## 2. Official button map

From the official Quest 2, Quest 3 and PS VR2 diagrams. Quest labels first, PS VR2 in brackets.

| Input | Official label | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Trigger** (R2 / L2) | Center on ball | Warps you to your ball and lines you up. Works while the ball is rolling. Also the button for every menu interaction. |
| **Grip** (R1 / L1) | Grip To Putt (optional) & keep ball alive when out of bounds | With Grip To Putt on, the head is only solid while you hold grip. Holding grip when the ball leaves the course keeps it in play instead of resetting. |
| **Thumbstick up** | Teleport / Move (point straight up to fly) | Push forward to teleport (aim, blue circle, release) or walk, per the Movement setting. Point the controller at the sky and hold up to fly; the controller rumbles when flight starts, and you fly where the putter points. Trigger near the ground lands you *(community)*. |
| **Thumbstick left / right** | Turn | Snap or smooth turn. Turning and moving share one stick. |
| **Thumbstick down** | Pick up Lost Ball & Undo Stroke (Practice Mode) | Aim the putter at a lost ball or foxhunt item and pull back to vacuum it in. In Practice mode, pull back after a shot to undo it. On Welcome Island, pull back to pick up a practice ball; reach over your shoulder and pull back for a new one *(community)*. |
| **X / A** (Cross / Square) | Pause Menu | Opens the scorecard and menus. Settings cog is top right. The glossary also describes "God Mode": at the end of a course or on A you hover above the course and look huge to others. |
| **Y / B** (Circle / Triangle) | Instant Replay | Replays your most recent shot for you and everyone in the room. |
| **Hold Oculus / Universal Menu button** (Options) | Hold to recenter | Recenters the view. Note: on Quest, holding Oculus and clicking trigger takes a screenshot of the scorecard before the recenter ring completes (Steam FAQ). |
| **Tilt your wrist** | Wristwatch | Local time, hole, strokes, par, round. Also holds the mic toggle, the Mulligan button (Oct 2026), hole records (May 2026), the current foxhunt clue, and the CocoVision camera button. Scorecards themselves run on UTC. |

One contradiction in the official material: two knowledge base articles say Grip To Putt uses the trigger, but all three official controller diagrams, the Steam FAQ and every player guide say the grip button. Trust the diagrams.

**Hand tracking** (Quest, experimental, v6.8, 8 Oct 2026). Enable at system level (Settings > Movement Tracking > Hands and Controllers) and in game (Settings > Options > Hand Tracking Experimental; a tutorial runs first). Two styles:
- *Swing Mode*: grip your hand as if holding a putter and swing. Making a fist works as Grip To Putt.
- *Pinch-to-Putt* (the recommended style, same as the retired phone edition): pinch the ball, pull back to raise a power meter, aim, release. Enter it from the radial gesture menu by pinching and releasing in the lower right corner.
- Pinch and swipe to turn in place; pinch and pull down to teleport to the ball *(press)*. Flying is disabled. A lefty toggle sits on the same Options page. Pick a controller up to switch back. A hand icon appears by your name on the scorecard and the round is marked with a hand-tracking modifier, but unlike mulligans it does not void records.

## 3. A round, step by step

1. **Welcome Island.** You spawn on the island. The **Shack** holds every owned and buyable course, your avatar and profile (several profiles per install), the Friends tab under your avatar, settings, and the Quick Match / Private Room choice. The counter in front has the ball tray; ball trails and hole celebrations are to its left (3 of each in the base game).
2. **Pick a course.** Each icon shows a description and **Front 9, Back 9 or full 18**. Hard versions appear once unlocked. Course leaderboards sit under each course heading in the hut *(community)*. Mulligan count and modifiers (Mini, Giant, gravity) are set here in the course options or later in the expanded scorecard.
3. **Address the ball.** Trigger. The shaft resizes to your posture; the head stays at ball height. Stand square to your line, head over the ball, knees soft. Swing from the shoulders as a pendulum, power from backswing length, face square through impact. There is no spin from the stroke; spin comes only from surfaces and bounces.
4. **Practice swings.** With Grip To Putt on, swing through the ball along your real line with grip released. Or step back a pace.
5. **After the shot.** Trigger to follow the ball (even while it rolls), trigger again when it stops to stand behind it. Or turn on **Auto Teleport**. Yellow Ball Locator arrows point to an off-screen ball. Y/B replays the shot.
6. **Out of bounds.** Water, overshooting the green or leaving the course resets the ball to its last lie *(unverified: "respawns in front of you", PSNProfiles)*. No source documents a penalty stroke. Hold grip as the ball goes out to keep it alive instead.
7. **Stroke Limit** (on by default): last allowed stroke is triple bogey; miss it and a quadruple bogey is recorded and you move on. Off: putt "to your heart's desire".
8. **Next hole.** Your ball waits at the next tee; trigger takes you there. Honors order by default, but nobody is blocked from moving ahead. Notifications call out birdies, eagles and stroke-limit events (toggle in Options).
9. **Scorecard quirks.** The card does not update while open and cannot be refreshed after the round ends *(community, 2021 review)*. Tutorial popups toggle via the graduation cap on the scorecard.

## 4. Scouting and tips

- Teleport ahead or fly above the hole before putting; many landing zones are hidden from the tee. Treasures also show on the overview map, tiny *(community)*.
- Use the putter as a ruler: hold it up to draw the line from ball to hole, then pick a pebble or texture dot on that line to aim at *(community)*.
- Raising **Strength** makes the same swing travel farther ("the ball will be lighter"). Default 50%; 40% if greens feel hot, 90% for a real-putter feel, 100% for very long shots *(community)*.
- The hole is a little under 11 cm wide, two and a half balls; the harder you putt the more centred you must be *(community guide's figure)*.
- The club still contacts the ball through thin barrier walls *(community)*.
- Practice mode: no scoring, holes in any order, stick-back undo, click a hole number on the scorecard map to warp there. Arizona Modern hole 18 and the Welcome Island practice green are the suggested ranges *(community)*.

## 5. Settings reference

Entry points: the Shack settings menu, and the scorecard cog during a round. Tabs as the Steam guide and official articles name them. The Grip To Putt checkbox is also on the primary settings screen (2026 new-player guide).

**Putter**: Strength; Angle of the club head relative to the controller; Grip To Putt; Invert Hands; Custom Grip / calibration for physical attachments (FormX Grip-to-Putter 4 ships an in-game preset; restored on Steam in 2026).
**Movement**: Teleport (blink) or Smooth locomotion; snap turn with angle, or smooth turn with speed; Lock Ball Position; Auto Teleport.
**Audio**: effects, music, voice chat volumes; Mute box.
**Options**: Stroke Limit; Ball Locator; Ball Trail; Nametags; Notifications; Language (English, French, Spanish, German, Chinese, Japanese, Korean); Hand Tracking Experimental and its lefty toggle. *(The fan wiki files Locator, Trail and Nametags under a "Visuals" tab and lists a "Putter Tracking Guard"; unverified.)*
**Accessibility**: Magnifier; larger wristwatch; Controller Smoothing. Course comfort toggles: El Dorado Hard lightning, Widow's Walkabout spiders, Laser Lair laser flicker, Alice scaling comfort (delayed scaling, blink transition).
**Experimental**: Lay Down mode (turn off if tracking glitches).
**PS VR2 only**: Crossplay toggle at the Shack (off locks you to PS VR2 players in Quick Match and Private Room); profanity filter on names and room names; PS5 Activities for Quick and Private Match.
**Putter head rule**: with Grip To Putt off, the head goes dead whenever you aren't looking at the ball, to stop accidental putts.

## 6. Multiplayer, step by step

- **Private Room**: Shack > Private Room, type a room name, share it. Anyone on any platform who types the same name joins (names are not owned and can be reused later). Host = whoever created the room; host picks courses and mode. Up to 8 players. Host can kick (bars re-entry) and block (bars them from every room that host opens) since Oct 2026. A "Rejoin Round" button appears after a network drop.
- **Friends** (since Alice, Dec 2025): Friends tab under your avatar at the Shack. You can only befriend someone you have shared a private match with (they appear in Recently Played, last 50). 50 friends, 10 pending requests, 50 blocked. Green dot = friend online, blue = request. Invites start a no-code private round; the studio recommends this over room names for real privacy.
- **Guest Pass**: the host's owned DLC courses can be played by non-owners in the room, but guests cannot collect lost balls or do foxhunts.
- **Quick Match**: 1v1 on a Front or Back 9. Since the Blokhaven update (May 2026) you pick from a 3x3 grid: three base courses, three DLC courses, three specialty options (Race, Closest Putt, Mini/Giant), easy or hard, with a language filter, an indicator for courses where someone is already waiting, and "First Available". After the match you rate your opponent. Abandoning an opponent draws warnings and then bans.
- **Modes**: Standard (strokes added, lowest wins); Match Play (a point per hole to the lowest score, ties share; leavers cannot rejoin); Race (everyone at once against the clock, strokes don't count, first to finish the last hole wins; late joiners become observers); Practice; Custom (choose mode, holes, turn order); Mini Mode and Giant Mode (player scale changes, world and ball stay normal). Turn orders: Honors (default), Distance (farthest putts first), Simultaneous (not in Match Play).
- **Balls never collide between players**: "Due to technical and design constraints, we don't have any current plans for supporting real-time multiplayer ball collisions" (studio, June 2024).
- **Mulligans** (Oct 2026): host sets a per-player count in the course options or the expanded scorecard; after a putt, look at your wrist and press Mulligan. Mulligans void records.
- **Voice**: always on in rooms, cross-platform, never recorded. Mute yourself in three places: the wristwatch mic toggle, the checkbox at the bottom right of the Private Room player list, or the scorecard. Mute others via the audio icon beside their name in the player list or scorecard. Blocked players show as "Blocked Player", are auto-muted both ways and never appear in your Quick Matches. On Quest the mic permission must be granted even if you never talk.
- **Observing**: no formal spectator mode. Join after everyone has entered the course and you're an observer; trigger during someone's turn puts you behind them *(community)*. Tournament hosts use this for streaming.

## 7. Collecting and unlocking

- **Lost balls**: 18 per regular course, one per hole; aim and pull the stick back. Checkmarks on the scorecard's bottom row; found balls go into the Pro Shop tray. Built to be reachable seated with smooth locomotion; flying is the "edge".
- **Hard mode**: per course, unlocked by finding 10 of the 18 lost balls or making par on the regular course *(community sources, including the studio's Facebook; no official page fetched states the rule)*. Hard courses are redesigned, usually night, layouts. The Homestar Runner pack's course has no Hard mode.
- **Foxhunt**: near hole 1 of each Hard course an arrow points at the first item; pick it up like a lost ball, read the next clue on your wristwatch, follow the chain; reward is a commemorative putter equipped at the Shack.
- **Cosmetics**: 3 ball trails and 3 hole celebrations in the base game, more in $2.99 Cosmetic Packs and course packs; a promo-code redemption system (Oct 2026) hands out trails via the newsletter. Trails can be disabled in settings.
- **Records**: hole records on the wristwatch; modifiers (Mini, Giant, gravity, mulligans) disable records, hand tracking does not.
- **Saves**: the studio's own cloud sync, per platform, no cross-save. Lost collectibles can be restored by support; lost scores cannot.
- **CocoVision camera** (Mar 2026, Quest/Rift/Steam): camera button on the wristwatch; snapshot with countdown or video; selfie or forward facing; lock it in the world or on your head; drag it by pointing and holding grip; 720p/1080p, 4K on PC.

## 8. Welcome Island things to do

- Click the **Driving Range** or **Practice Green** sign to switch them on. Range has targets, a hit counter and slingshots (slingshots, chess and Employee Mode are being scaled back in 2026).
- On the green you can pick up, lob and throw balls. Reach the top of the headland or ride the raft *(community)*.
- Limited-time course "takeovers" redress the island at each DLC launch.
