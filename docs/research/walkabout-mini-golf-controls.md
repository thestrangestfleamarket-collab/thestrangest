# Walkabout Mini Golf: how to play, controls and settings

Companion to `walkabout-mini-golf.md`. Compiled 2 Nov 2026 from search-result snippets of the official Mighty Coconut resources page and support knowledge base, the Steam community FAQ and guides, the fan wiki (walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com), PSNProfiles, VR Lowdown, Maecker VR and press coverage. Direct page reads were blocked in this workspace, so exact menu wording should be checked in the game; conflicts between sources are marked.

## 1. The one-controller model

- The whole game is played with **one controller**, by design ("we prioritised accessibility, hence one controller for gameplay, which limits button mapping options", developer reply on Steam). Movement and turning share that controller's thumbstick.
- The idle second controller can confuse tracking. Mighty Coconut says to put it "well outside your Guardian Boundary or outside the room". On PS VR2 you can turn the spare Sense controller off by holding its PS button 10 seconds.
- To switch hands, pick up the other controller and press a button. If you putt with the "wrong" controller (lefty on the right controller or vice versa), turn on **Invert Hands** so the trigger snaps you to the correct side of the ball.
- PS VR2 catch: the Cross button that reopens the game lives only on the right controller, so a lefty who turns the right one off cannot get back in. Official workarounds: re-enter with a DualSense, or putt with the right controller plus Invert Hands.

## 2. Button map (Quest / PC VR, PS VR2 equivalents in brackets)

| Input | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** (R2/L2) | Teleport to your ball. Press again once the ball has stopped to snap into putting position behind it. Works while the ball is still rolling. In flight, trigger near the ground lands you. |
| **Grip** | With **Grip To Putt** on, hold it to make the putter head solid. Release it to swing straight through the ball for practice swings. (Mighty Coconut's own FAQ text once said "trigger"; VR Lowdown, the Steam FAQ and players say grip. Grip is the current behaviour.) |
| **A / X** (Cross) | Open the scorecard. Settings cog is top right of it. |
| **Menu button** (X on right Sense, per a PS VR2 review) | Eagle-eye overview of the island/course. |
| **Thumbstick push forward** | Walk (smooth) or blink teleport, per your Movement setting. |
| **Thumbstick left / right** | Snap or smooth turn (angle or speed adjustable). |
| **Thumbstick pull back / down** toward you, aiming the putter at a lost ball or foxhunt clue | Vacuums the item into your collection. Same gesture to pick up foxhunt items. |
| **Thumbstick pull back** after a shot, Practice mode only | Undo the stroke (ball returns to its previous lie). |
| **Point the putter at the sky and hold the thumbstick up** a few seconds until the controller rumbles | **Flyabout** flight. Keep holding up and fly wherever the putter points; slow, comfortable. Trigger near ground level to land. The game never explains this. |
| **Tilt your wrist** (the controller hand) | Read the wristwatch: hole number, strokes, par, round, local time. Mulligan button is on the watch (Oct 2026). |
| **Pinch gestures** (Quest hand tracking, experimental, v6.8) | Pinch and move hand left/right to snap turn, pull down to teleport to ball, pinch the ball and pull back for a power meter then release to putt. A second "grip" style mimics holding a putter. Radial menu via pinch-release in the lower right. |

## 3. A round, step by step

1. **Welcome Island.** You spawn at the island. The **Shack** (Pro Shop) is the hub: pick solo, **Quick Match** or **Private Room**; pick a ball and putter from the tray on the counter (ball trails and hole celebrations are to the left of the ball tray); click your avatar or the cog for settings.
2. **Pick a course.** Each course icon shows a description and the choice of **Front 9, Back 9 or full 18**, regular or Hard (Hard appears once unlocked). Course leaderboards sit under each course heading in the hut. A fresh install today shows 14 included courses, 6 putters and 8 balls.
3. **Tee off.** Press trigger to stand at your ball. The putter shaft sizes itself to your posture: crouch and it shortens, stand tall and it lengthens, the head stays at ball height. Swing from the shoulders in a pendulum, power from backswing length, not wrist flick (official FAQ and every tips guide agree). There is no spin; the ball only picks up spin from surfaces and bounces.
4. **After the shot.** Tap trigger to follow the ball, tap again when it stops. Or turn on **Auto Teleport** and the game moves you. Yellow **Ball Locator** arrows point to the ball when it is off screen.
5. **Trouble.** Water, overshooting the green or leaving the course puts the ball out of bounds; it respawns at its last lie in front of you. No penalty stroke is documented anywhere, only the wasted stroke.
6. **Stroke limit.** With **Stroke Limit** on (default), your last allowed stroke on a hole is triple bogey; miss it and a quadruple bogey is recorded and you advance. Turn it off in the scorecard's Course tab to keep hitting.
7. **Next hole.** Your ball waits at the next tee; you don't have to wait for slower players (Honors order by default). Scorecard notifications call out birdies, eagles and stroke-limit events (toggle under Options > Notifications).
8. **Scorecard quirks.** The card does not update its tally while open, and cannot be refreshed after the round ends (reviewer note). Scorecards use UTC.

## 4. Scouting and reading a hole

- Teleport ahead to where you intend to land, or Flyabout above the hole, before you putt. Many holes hide the landing zone from the tee (PSNProfiles, VR Lowdown).
- Take practice swings through the ball with Grip To Putt off-grip to rehearse the line.
- Keep your head still through the stroke. Raising **Strength** makes the ball "lighter" (same swing goes farther); default 50%, 40% if greens feel hot, 90% for a real-putter feel, 100% for very long shots.
- The club still makes contact through thin barrier walls (The Lady M fan guide).
- Practice mode: no scoring, play holes in any order, undo any shot with stick-back, click a hole number on the scorecard map to warp there.

## 5. Settings reference

Two entry points: the Shack (click your avatar or the cog) and the scorecard cog during a round. Tabs as the fan wiki lists them:

**Putter**
- **Grip To Putt**: head solid only while grip is held. Recommended by the studio.
- **Strength**: swing multiplier, default 50%.
- **Angle**: tilts the putter head relative to the controller.
- **Custom Grip / Custom Calibration**: calibrates the virtual putter to a physical attachment (tilt and rotation; also changes the pointer angle). Official HelloReal "Grip-to-Putter" weighted shaft has its own preset.
- **Invert Hands**: lefty/righty swap.
- **Putter Tracking Guard**: disables the head when tracking is poor, to stop wild putts.

**Movement**
- **Teleport (blink)** or **Smooth** movement; thumbstick relative to controller or to headset (player preference threads).
- **Turning**: snap with adjustable angle or smooth with adjustable speed.
- **Auto Teleport**: move to the ball when it stops (built for putter attachments and seated players).
- **Lock Ball Position**: the ball is always placed at the centre of your play area, so trigger puts you in the exact same spot every time. Recommended for seated and stationary play.

**Visuals**
- **Nametags** on/off (the wiki suggests everyone off for hide-and-seek).
- **Ball Locator** arrows on/off.
- **Ball Trail** on/off.

**Options**
- **Stroke Limit** on/off.
- **Notifications** (birdie, eagle, stroke-limit alerts) on/off.
- **Language**.
- **Experimental Hand Tracking** (Quest, off by default, runs a tutorial; also needs hand tracking on at system level).
- **Tutorial** popups: graduation-cap toggle on the scorecard.

**Accessibility** (added later, in the Shack settings and the scorecard settings)
- **Magnifier**, **larger Watch**, **Controller Smoothing** (eases thumbstick acceleration).
- Experimental **Lay Down** mode (disable if tracking glitches).

**Audio / multiplayer**
- **Mute** yourself: Mute box on the scorecard or at the Shack, or the headset's own mic button / PS Quick Settings.
- **Mute a player**: audio icon beside their name in the Private Room player list, or on the course scorecard.
- Push-to-talk does not exist (Steam thread).
- On Quest, the microphone permission must be granted even if you never talk; the studio says a denied permission can cause gameplay and graphics issues.

## 6. Multiplayer, step by step

- **Private Room**: at the Shack choose Private Room, type a room name, share the name. Friends on any platform type the same name to join. Whoever has been in the room longest is host and picks courses and mode. Up to 8 players. Anyone with the name can join, so the official advice for a closed game is to use the friends list (50 friends, 10 pending requests) and invite directly.
- **Guest Pass**: courses the host owns and marks as Guest Pass are playable by non-owners in that room, but guests cannot collect lost balls or do foxhunts.
- **Quick Match**: pairs you with one other player on a random base course's Front or Back 9; regular courses only, no Hard.
- **Modes** (June 2024): **Standard** stroke play; **Match Play** (a point per hole to the lowest score, ties share, leavers cannot rejoin); **Race** (everyone at once against the clock, strokes do not count, first to finish the last hole wins, late joiners become observers); **Practice**; **Custom** (choose mode, holes and turn order). Turn order **Honors** (default), **Distance** (farthest putts first) or **Simultaneous** (not in Match Play).
- **Mulligans** (v6.8, Oct 2026): host sets a per-player count before or mid-round via a scorecard modifier; press the mulligan button on your watch to retake a shot.
- **Moderation**: host can kick; blocking a player mutes them permanently, keeps them out of every room you host and out of your Quick Matches (block list 50). Code of Conduct: warnings, temporary suspension, permanent online ban.

## 7. Collecting and unlocking

- **Lost balls**: one hidden per hole on every regular course (18). Point the putter at it and pull the thumbstick back; it is vacuumed in. Progress on the scorecard's bottom row; found balls appear in the Pro Shop. Designed to be reachable seated with smooth locomotion.
- **Hard mode**: per course, unlocked by collecting 10 of the 18 lost balls or by making par on the regular course (sources differ between "at par" and "under par"). Hard courses are redesigned night layouts, not just harder pars.
- **Foxhunt**: on each Hard course, near hole 1 an arrow points at the first clue item; pick it up like a lost ball, read the current clue on your wristwatch, follow the chain. Reward: a commemorative putter, equipped at the Shack. Some courses have two or three hunts.
- **Staff doors** (Welcome Island easter egg): doors with red lights have props and sometimes codes visible through the window; match the door to a course, play that course's Hard version, find the door from the other side and open it (Alfheim: past hole 14, a rune code spelling "par"). The double doors under the Pro Shop do not open.
- **Cosmetics**: ball trails and hole celebrations are selected at the Shack; the Strong Bad pack lists 3 hole celebrations (1 unlocked, 2 to earn). Unlock conditions for celebrations are not documented.
- **Modifiers** (Mini/Giant, gravity, slingshots) disable course records while on.
- Lost cloud saves: support@mightycoconut.com will unlock all balls and clubs.

## 8. Welcome Island things to do

- **Driving range**: targets, hit counter, slingshot targets too.
- **38-hole putting green**: pick up and throw balls; no documented way to jump the ball to a specific practice hole (a player request thread).
- Pool, raft tour through a cave, chess boards (being scaled back in 2026), hidden developer break room.
