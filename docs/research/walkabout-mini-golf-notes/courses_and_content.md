## Key question 1: Full list of courses as of 2026 (base + DLC, themes, licensed IPs, dates, prices, distinctive holes)

### Takeaway
As of October 2026 there are 42 numbered courses (8 original free courses + 34 DLC courses, 6 of which were folded into the base game on 1 May 2025 to make 14 "included" courses), plus the Homestar Runner Distraction Pack (June 2026) whose "Bubs' Many Golfs" course in Strong Badia the fan wiki counts as a 43rd course. DLC courses have historically cost $3.99 (one early one $2.99); the 2026 courses are $4.99.

## Key question 2: Course structure (18 holes, par, easy/hard, night) and how hard mode is unlocked

### Takeaway
Each course is 18 holes with its own par; each (except Strong Badia) has a regular version and a redesigned hard version set at night. Hard mode is unlocked per course by collecting 10 of the regular course's 18 lost balls or by scoring at/under par, whichever comes first.

### Cited Findings
- Developer post: "there are 18 lost balls on every #WalkaboutMiniGolf regular course. The bottom row of the scorecard will show which ones you've found. Collect 10 to unlock Hard Mode!" — [Walkabout Mini Golf on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/walkaboutminigolf/photos/a.123547836545882/130001192567213/?type=3)
- UploadVR review: find 10 hidden balls on a course, or score below par, to unlock a "hard" version "with redesigned holes and new lighting" — [UploadVR review](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-review/)
- A fan guide: "The Night Courses unlock as you either find 10 of the 18 lost balls or beat Par" — [The Lady M](https://theladymonline.yolasite.com/walkabout-mini-golf.php); the fan wiki says making par works — [Welcome Island (fandom)](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Welcome_Island)
- Wikipedia (via snippet): the harder version is set at night and unlocks new clubs on completion — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walkabout_Mini_Golf)
- Official store copy: "unlock Hard Mode for more challenging versions of all courses" — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1408230/Walkabout_Mini_Golf_VR/)
- A Steam user: hard mode maps are "a different map in general, not just harder holes" — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/4/4038101970208920430)

- Game modes added over time: slingshot target areas on some courses (Aug 2024); Mini mode (player shrunk) and Giant mode (player enlarged; first on May 28, 2025 for Cherry Blossom, Sweetopia, Temple at Zerzura, Venice); a Low/Normal/High gravity modifier; course records are not saved while modifiers are active — [Mighty Coconut resources](https://www.mightycoconut.com/wmg-resources); [Category:Giant Mode (fandom)](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Giant_Mode); [Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/0/599643705297635607)

## Key question 3: Lost Balls (count, finding, reward, hidden-ball mechanics)

### Takeaway
Every regular course hides one lost ball per hole (18 per course); you collect one by walking up to it and pulling/holding the thumbstick down, it becomes a custom ball selectable in the Pro Shop, and the scorecard's bottom row tracks them. Guests playing on a Guest Pass cannot collect them.

### Cited Findings
- 18 lost balls per regular course, tracked on the scorecard's bottom row — [Walkabout Mini Golf on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/walkaboutminigolf/photos/a.123547836545882/130001192567213/?type=3)
- "a hidden ball at every hole in each Walkabout course"; press the analog stick downward to vacuum it into your collection — [UploadVR review](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-review/)
- "walk up to them and hold the right stick down" — [Welcome Island (fandom)](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Welcome_Island)
- Collected balls and clubs appear in the Pro Shop: "as you collect lost balls from the regular courses and golf clubs from the hard courses, they will appear here"; at the start there are 14 courses, 6 clubs and 8 balls — [Welcome Island (fandom)](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Welcome_Island)
- Reviews count the base-game balls differently: "188 literal easter eggs" in one; "150-plus custom collecti-balls" in another; "each regular level hides 18 unique lost balls, one per hole" in a third — [Game Chronicles](https://gamechronicles.com/walkabout-mini-golf-review-quest-3); [Push Square](https://pushsquare.com/reviews/psvr2/walkabout-mini-golf); [Road to VR](https://www.roadtovr.com/?p=114707)

### Gaps

## Key question 4: Fox Hunt and custom putters

### Takeaway
Each hard course has a Fox Hunt: a clue chain started near hole 1, with the current clue shown on your wrist/watch; finishing it earns one commemorative putter for that course (Laser Lair has two hunts/two putters; Myst reportedly three putters), displayed next to the other putters in the Pro Shop.

### Cited Findings
- Glossary: a foxhunt is a clue-driven scavenger hunt in each course's hard mode, "after-which you earn a commemorative putter to use in-game" — [Mighty Coconut glossary](https://www.mightycoconut.com/wmg-glossary)
- "On hard courses, there's now a clue leading you on a treasure hunt. Called fox hunts, you can look at your wrist to study the next clue. If you find every clue, you unlock a specially-designed putter you can equip back at the welcome shack" — [UploadVR review](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-review/)
- To start, look near the first hole and pick up the item the starting arrow points to; the current clue is on your wristwatch — [Welcome Island (fandom)](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Welcome_Island); [PSFanatic](https://www.psfanatic.com/walkabout-mini-golf-ps-vr2-tourist-trap-foxhunt-guide-get-a-new-putter/)
- Laser Lair: "This hard course features two foxhunts ... collect 2 putters" — [Laser Lair (fandom)](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Laser_Lair)
- Myst has three putters per a user post; the same thread gives totals of "23 different putters overall, not counting the default ones" and "26 non-starter putters" (2021–2023 posts, outdated) — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/4/4041481663548652915)
- Named putters: Captain Fawkes' Saber (Tourist Trap); the Goblin King's clawed putter (Labyrinth) — [PSFanatic](https://www.psfanatic.com/walkabout-mini-golf-ps-vr2-tourist-trap-foxhunt-guide-get-a-new-putter/); [Labyrinth (fandom)](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Labyrinth)

- Guests on a shared DLC cannot take part in foxhunts — [Mighty Coconut glossary](https://www.mightycoconut.com/wmg-glossary)

### Inferences
- With ~41 hard courses plus Laser Lair's extra and Myst's extras, the putter count by late 2026 is well above the 23–26 reported in 2023; no current total was found.

### Gaps
- No "Golden Putter" was found in any source; no complete current list of putters was retrieved.
- No source confirms a putter tied to finishing hard mode under par; all sources tie putters to completing the fox hunt.

## Key question 5: Other hidden content (secret areas, easter eggs, hidden holes, clubhouse, seasonal content)

### Takeaway
The lobby is Welcome Island (Pro Shop shack, driving range, putting areas) with a hidden developer break room under the shack; courses are seeded with recurring easter eggs (skulls, mini-skulls, bananas, Mighty Coconut references, hidden skeletons, staff doors), and Holiday Hideaway has a hidden hole 18. No time-limited or removed content was found.

### Cited Findings
- Welcome Island is the main menu area, with a driving range and putting areas; the Pro Shop is where you pick course, club and ball — [Steam announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/1408230/announcements/detail/3204883492736356996); [Welcome Island (fandom)](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Welcome_Island)

- The fan wiki's Easter Eggs table tracks tiny skulls/skeletons, bananas and Mighty Coconut references per course (e.g. Tiki à Coco, Hollywood, Gloop Lair, Blokhaven have skulls; Strong Badia none) — [WMG Easter Eggs (fandom)](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Easter_Eggs); a YouTube compilation covers "ALL Easter Eggs & Hidden Bodies" — [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_CpGeqqWCAo)

## Key question 6: How courses are sold/delivered (free updates vs DLC, bundles, "Course Pass"), platforms

### Takeaway
Courses ship as individual paid DLC ($2.99–$3.99 historically, $4.99 for 2026 releases), with free updates for remasters and game modes; on 1 May 2025 six DLC courses were folded into the base game and its price rose from $14.99 to $24.99. The "Whole in One" bundle collects everything; no "Course Pass" product exists in any source found.
