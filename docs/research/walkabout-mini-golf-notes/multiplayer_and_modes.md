# Walkabout Mini Golf: multiplayer, social and game modes (Mighty Coconut)

Research note on sourcing: in this sandbox every direct page fetch failed (mightycoconut.com, support.mightycoconut.com, store.steampowered.com, roadtovr.com, guildorder.com, androidcentral.com all refused to resolve), so every finding below comes from search-result snippets of those pages, not full-page reads. Snippet text is quoted as returned; treat finer details as "per snippet". Third-party fan wikis (guildorder.com, ingamenews.com) are marked as unofficial wherever used. Current as of 2 Nov 2026.

## Multiplayer structure: rooms, codes, player counts, cross-play, host controls, how others appear

### Takeaway
Walkabout has exactly three ways to play: solo, a 1v1 "Quick Match" against a random opponent, and a "Private Room" of up to 8 players joined by a shared room name/code, which the host (whoever creates the room) controls, picking courses and game mode. The cap was 5 until the Labyrinth update on 28 Jul 2022 raised it to 8. Cross-play spans Quest, Steam/PC VR, PSVR2, Pico (and, until May 2026, the iPhone Pocket Edition); there is no Apple Vision Pro version and no public/open rooms.

### Inferences
- Rooms are name-keyed, not numerically coded: "room code" and "room name" are used interchangeably in official text; anyone who knows the name can join, which is why the fan guides push friend invites instead.
- There is no public matchmaking beyond the 1v1 Quick Match; group play with strangers happens through community Discords, not in-game browsing.
- The default round appears to be everyone-at-own-pace on the same hole sequence (simultaneous), with strict turn order only an option in Custom mode; the eight-player "chase balls simultaneously" wording supports this.

### Gaps
- Whether other players' balls collide with yours or pass through (ghost balls) is not stated in any source found; the glossary's God Mode wording ("avoid accidentally hitting your ball") hints that balls are physical by default, but this is unconfirmed.
- No source found on whether there is an in-game friends list separate from platform (Meta/PSN/Steam) friends, or on cross-platform friend adding.
- Exact date and content of "patch 6.8" (host kick/block) not confirmed by an official source.

## Communication: voice, mute, gestures, text

### Takeaway
Voice chat is built into every Private Room across all platforms; players can mute individuals from the player list (audio icon) or the scorecard. No proximity/spatial voice, text chat, emotes, megaphone or pointer feature was found in any source.

### Cited Findings
- "up to 8 players join a private room with voice chat no matter what platform" — [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1408230/Walkabout_Mini_Golf_VR/)
- Official support: in a Private Room the player list has an audio icon beside each name to mute that player; players can also mute themselves or others from the course scorecard — [support.mightycoconut.com, microphone troubleshooting](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/article/41-microphone-troubleshooting/)
- Quest mic permission must be ON for Walkabout; turned off "can cause gameplay and graphical issues within our game, even if you do not utilize your in-game mic" — [support.mightycoconut.com, Quest microphone](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/article/41-quest-microphone-multiplayer)
- The mute-player option arrived with the Welcome Island update (Apr 2022), alongside a new options menu — [Android Central](https://androidcentral.com/gaming/virtual-reality/walkabout-mini-golf-vr-adds-new-social-space-with-driving-range-and-more)
- Steam community FAQ: an observer "might want to turn off their mic in-game with the toggle on the scorecard so the players don't hear it" — [Steam community FAQ](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/4/3187986750504779517)
- Avatars have lip sync ("Customizable avatars featuring lip sync") — [PlayStation page](https://www.playstation.com/en-hk/games/walkabout-mini-golf)

### Inferences
- The scorecard doubles as the in-round social panel (mute, player list), which fits a game whose UI is deliberately sparse.

### Gaps
- No source states whether voice is room-wide or distance-attenuated; no "walkie-talkie"/megaphone feature was found (searches returned only other products).
- No text chat, emote, gesture or laser-pointer feature was found; absence of evidence, not confirmation of absence.

## Game modes beyond a plain round

### Takeaway
Official modes are Standard, Match Play, Race, Practice and Custom (holes, turn order, scoring), plus per-course Hard/Night versions, modifiers (Mini Mode, low gravity), host-set Mulligans (Oct 2026) and the collectible hunts (lost balls on easy, fox hunts on hard). A 2024 core-code rewrite shipped the new modes with the remastered Cherry Blossom course.

### Cited Findings
- Official KB lists Standard, Match Play, Race, Practice and Custom; "Custom lets users customize a round by adjusting holes, turn order, and scoring" — [support.mightycoconut.com, Game Modes](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/article/12-game-modes/); glossary: Custom lets you "choose your own game mode, holes and turn order" — [Mighty Coconut glossary](https://mightycoconut.com/wmg-glossary)
- Match mode: "a point is awarded for the lowest score on a hole" — [Mixed-News](https://mixed-news.com/en/walkabout-mini-golf-major-update-brings-driving-range-and-more/); Race: all play at once, fastest to finish — [Mixed-News](https://mixed-news.com/en/walkabout-mini-golf-redesigned-cherry-blossom-course-and-new-game-modes/)

- Night/Hard mode: "Night Mode isn't just cosmetic, every hole has been modified, and sometimes completely redesigned to provide an entirely new course"; Steam dev post covers "Mini Mode and low-gravity modifiers" — [PlayStation page](https://playstation.com/en-bg/games/walkabout-mini-golf); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/0/599643705297635607); DLC courses ship "both easy and hard versions, 18 new lost balls, fox hunt" — [UploadVR, Labyrinth](https://uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-labyrinth-release-date/)

## Competitive play: tournaments, leaderboards, records, stats

### Takeaway
Mighty Coconut deliberately ships no global leaderboards and runs no tournaments itself; competition lives in community leagues (Discord, e.g. Classic Golf League, Walkabout Weekly) sanctioned through a "Walkabout Recognized Tournament" application, plus occasional studio-backed events such as the Sep 2023 arcade Free Week tournament.

### Cited Findings
- "the studio has intentionally not built global leaderboards to emphasize personal enjoyment and individual progression over sheer competition" — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/mighty-coconut-unveils-alices-adventures-in-wonderland-course-for-walkabout-mini-golf/)
- Recognized Tournaments: Mighty Coconut "does not organize tournaments but is happy to have them hosted on its game"; organisers must file the Walkabout Mini Golf Recognized Tournament application, agree to its Tournament Terms of Service, label events "Walkabout Recognized Tournament", keep the studio updated on registration, start and winner, and not disparage the studio — [mightycoconut.com/wmg-tournaments](https://mightycoconut.com/wmg-tournaments)
- "regular tournaments are run through the Discord server" — [MobyGames](https://www.mobygames.com/game/167763)
- Free Week & Tournament (arcades, 1–2 Sep 2023): prizes "to the best 10 global scorers from the arcades, verified by an in-headset screenshot" — [Mighty Coconut blog](https://mightycoconut.com/blog/free-week)
- Studio calendar lists community events such as a beginners' 18+ tournament — [Mighty Coconut calendar](https://mightycoconut.com/wmg-calendar/walkabout-mini-golf-for-beginners-aged-18-tournament); community "Walkabout Mini Golf Tour" events — [No Proscenium](https://www.noproscenium.com/community-event-walkabout-mini-golf-tour-april/)
- Community leagues (unofficial sources): "Walkabout Weekly" runs global brackets and leaderboards with player stats; the Classic Golf League runs weekly tournaments with Elo ratings and seasons and claims 1,800+ matches per week on Discord; finishing order from rounds of up to eight players feeds ratings; season knockouts use single/double elimination — [Guild Order (unofficial)](https://guildorder.com/games/walkabout/guides/starting-a-walkabout-league); [Guild Order wiki (unofficial)](https://guildorder.com/games/walkabout/wiki/ratings-and-seasons-for-a-mini-golf-league); [CGL YouTube channel info](https://vling.net/channel/UCT96u7Kv0b4zzHGH13TiSTw/channel-info)
- Esports Earnings lists Walkabout Mini Golf prize-money players — [Esports Earnings](https://www.esportsearnings.com/games/802-walkabout-mini-golf/top-players-offline)

### Inferences
- With no official leaderboards, "course records" and "personal bests" are community-kept (league spreadsheets, screenshot verification), which is why the Recognized Tournament terms stress fairness rules.

### Gaps
- No source found on an in-game "Tournament Hub", weekly in-game tournaments, scorecard history or a personal-stats screen; searches for scorecard history returned nothing Walkabout-specific. Treat these as absent-or-unverified.
- Did not find the 2023 arcade tournament's winners or any later official tournament.

## Moderation and safety: reporting, blocking, age

### Takeaway
Online play is governed by a Code of Conduct with warnings, temporary suspensions and permanent bans from online features (alt accounts and false reports also sanctioned); hosts can mute, and per unofficial sources kick/block. Rated ESRB E (Users Interact, In-Game Purchases); PSVR2 hardware is 12+.

### Cited Findings
- Code of Conduct: playing means agreeing to the ToS and Code; "Failure to adhere to these standards may result in temporary or permanent suspension from the game's online features"; first/minor violations may draw a warning, severe or repeated ones a permanent ban from online and multiplayer; multiple accounts to evade penalties sanctions all linked accounts; filing false reports is a violation — [support.mightycoconut.com, Code of Conduct](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/article/44-code-of-conduct/) (page was marked preview when crawled)
- Mute via player-list audio icon or scorecard — [support.mightycoconut.com](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/article/41-microphone-troubleshooting/)
- Host kick and block (unofficial, "patch 6.8") — [ingamenews.com (unofficial)](https://ingamenews.com/games/walkabout-mini-golf-vr/guides/how-to-host-friends-and-share-dlc-with-guest-pass/)
- ESRB: E for Everyone, descriptors "Users Interact" and "In-Game Purchases", parental controls listed — [ESRB](https://www.esrb.org/ratings/40883/walkabout-mini-golf/); SpringboardVR (arcade listing) says 13+ — [SpringboardVR](https://springboardvr.com/marketplace/walkaboutminigolf); PSVR2 "isn't for use by children under the age of 12" — [PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/walkabout-mini-golf/)
- PSVR2 crossplay can be turned off (lets console players avoid other platforms' players) — [Mighty Coconut PSVR2 blog](https://mightycoconut.com/blog/psvr2)

### Inferences
- Because rooms are private-by-name and Quick Match is 1v1, exposure to strangers is limited by design; moderation tooling is correspondingly light (mute, host removal, account-level bans).
