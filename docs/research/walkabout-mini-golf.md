# Walkabout Builds Worlds Around One Honest Swing

Walkabout Mini Golf (Mighty Coconut, Quest launch 24 Sep 2020) is 18-hole stroke-play mini golf in which the putter is a single tracked controller and nothing else: there is no power meter, the ball takes its speed and direction from the real arm swing, scaled by a user-set **Strength** slider that defaults to 50%, and the putter head is only "live" while you hold the grip (**Grip To Putt**) or, with that off, while you are looking at the ball. The rules forgive everything except the stroke itself: par is read off a wristwatch, a **Stroke Limit** (on by default) ends a hole at triple bogey and records a quadruple, an out-of-bounds ball simply respawns, and since 8 Oct 2026 a host can hand out mulligans. Every course ships twice, a regular daytime 18 hiding one **lost ball** per hole and a redesigned night-time **Hard** 18 unlocked by collecting 10 of those balls or making par, whose clue-chain **Foxhunt** awards a commemorative putter; those two hunts are the whole progression system, because the studio deliberately ships no global leaderboards. By Oct 2026 there are **42 courses** (8 free, 34 DLC at $3.99, rising to $4.99 in 2026) released roughly every eight weeks with no battle pass, playable solo, in a 1v1 **Quick Match**, or in a **Private Room** of up to 8 players (5 until Jul 2022) joined by a shared room name with voice chat and cross-play across Quest, Steam, PS VR2 and Pico, in **Standard**, **Match Play**, **Race**, **Practice** or **Custom** modes. Founder Lucas Martell's stated philosophy is place first, golf second, with a standing rule that "it's okay to cheat the physics if it helps the player"; multiplayer, now the game's defining strength (over 55% of VR sessions), was added because Oculus pushed for it. Reception is uniformly strong (OpenCritic 88, Steam ~97–98% positive, Quest 4.8/5 over 13k+ ratings, 23 million hours and two billion putts in five years), but a shrinking flow of new VR buyers forced two rounds of layoffs in 2026 and a pivot toward a spin-off, Walkabout Dungeon Golfer, due summer 2027.

Quoted wording is as the search engine returned it, and exact figures should be re-checked against the Steam DLC page, the Mighty Coconut support knowledge base and the fan wiki before being relied on. ## The putter is one tracked controller, gated against false hits

The core verb is a real swing. The game "translates the speed and angle of a real arm swing into the ball's motion", and it uses only one controller, to the point that the idle second controller "can sometimes cause issues for tracking" ([VR & AR Wiki](https://vrarwiki.com/wiki/Walkabout_Mini_Golf); [Mighty Coconut support](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/tag/44)). A developer reply on Steam describes the stack as "Unity then PhysX with a bunch of other stuff on top", and Martell has said the forthcoming Dungeon Golfer "uses the same physics engine as Walkabout Mini Golf" ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/1/4038101970208943370/); [VR.org](https://vr.org/articles/walkabout-dungeon-golfer-mighty-coconut-roguelike-2027)). Martell told interviewers the team "obsessed over the physics of the sport" and named "the physics of putters, balls, and obstacles as well as the shared social experience" as the reason mini golf works in VR ([Minigolfnews](https://www.minigolfnews.com/?id=13907); [Checkpoint Gaming](https://checkpointgaming.net/features/2021/02/vr-corner-walkabout-mini-golf/)). No source describes the actual algorithm (how head velocity is sampled at contact, smoothing, velocity caps, friction or rail restitution), so a developer copying the feel is working from behaviour, not from a spec.

The swing is scaled, not clamped. The **Strength** setting "sets the power of your swing, which goes hand-in-hand with how hard you swing"; raising it "makes the ball lighter, in short", so the same swing travels farther ([Fandom Game Settings](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Settings); [Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2812327682)). The default is **50%**; a trophy guide recommends 40% if the ball runs hotter than expected and 100% for long shots, and one player reports that 90% approximates a real metal putter ([PSNProfiles](https://psnprofiles.com/guide/16777-walkabout-mini-golf-trophy-guide); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/1/4038101970208952482/)). Because the multiplier never caps the swing, a hard hit can still fly out of bounds, which is why an OB rule exists at all. The shaft needs no length slider: it is "continuously variable, so crouching shortens the shaft and standing on your toes lengthens it, while the putter head stays at the proper ball contact height" ([Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/1/4038101970208904819/)).

The main tuning problem the studio solved was not power mapping but false hits from a free-swinging tracked head, and it solved it twice. With **Grip To Putt** on, "the putter head is always visible, yet it only turns solid enough to strike the ball once the grip button is held", which lets players take practice swings straight through the ball along the shot line; the developers recommend turning it on under Scorecard > Settings ([VR Lowdown](https://vrlowdown.com/walkabout-mini-golf-tips/); [Steam FAQ](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/4/3187986750504779517)). With it off (the default), "the putter head deactivates when the player isn't looking at the ball, to prevent accidental putts" ([Mighty Coconut blog](https://www.mightycoconut.com/blog/glitching)). Feel is carried by the arm and the audio rather than rumble: Martell "recorded all of the original sounds of the ball rolling across different surfaces to capture the tactile feel", haptics are light enough that a Steam thread complains hard and soft strokes feel the same, and the official HelloReal "Grip-to-Putter" weighted shaft ships with an in-game preset and "native Grip-To-Putt integration" ([Voices of VR #1560](https://voicesofvr.com/1560-walkabout-mini-golfs-incredible-fusion-of-worldbuilding-gameplay-social-dynamics-dlc-experimentation/); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/0/804593328318443326/); [Mighty Coconut GTP](https://www.mightycoconut.com/gtp)). Reviewers judge the result "spot on" and say the studio "absolutely nailed the ball physics", though a 2025 review found it "95% great" with occasional odd bounces or balls stopping on slopes ([CogConnected](https://cogconnected.com/review/walkabout-mini-golf-review/); [6DOF Reviews](https://6dofreviews.com/reviews/games/walkabout-mini-golf/); [Spatial App Hub](https://spatialapphub.com/reviews/walkabout-mini-golf-review/)).

The studio has changed the ball's physics outright only once. Ice Lair (Mar 2024) runs the ball through an ice ray so it "becomes an ice cube", which Martell called "the first time we're massively changing the physics on the ball", built to make short putts less frustrating after "a lot of iteration", with hot plates on some holes to melt it back; the cube "slide[s] speedily over the ice patches" but "slows down quite a bit on grass" ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/ice-lair-tour-walkabout/); [PSFanatic](https://www.psfanatic.com/walkabout-mini-golf-ice-lair-review/)). Input is also widening: update 6.8 (8 Oct 2026) added **Experimental Hand Tracking** on Quest, off by default under Settings > Options with a tutorial, where you "pinch and move your hand right or left to snap turn and pull toward you to putt" ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-adds-mulligans-hand-tracking-a-new-dlc-course-today/); [MIXED](https://mixed-news.com/en/walkabout-mini-golf-planet-xylodon-experimental-hand-tracking-mulligans/)). The contrast case is instructive: the retired iPhone Pocket Edition needed a power bar and three putting styles (swipe-and-tap "Standard", gyroscope "Immersive", and a horizontal mode where you swing the phone) precisely because it had no tracked swing ([Mighty Coconut Pocket](https://mightycoconut.com/pocket)).

## Rules cost you nothing beyond the stroke

Scoring is plain stroke play: "Stroke play is the base mode", strokes are added across the round and the lowest total wins ([Mighty Coconut resources](https://www.mightycoconut.com/wmg-resources)). Every course is 18 holes, and the in-round UI is a wristwatch and a scorecard: "Tilt your wrist to view your virtual watch, and it will display local time along with the hole number, stroke count, par count, and round", while A/X opens the scorecard, whose Settings hold Grip To Putt and Strength and whose bottom row shows which lost balls you have found ([Mighty Coconut resources](https://www.mightycoconut.com/wmg-resources); [Steam FAQ](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/4/3187986750504779517); [Walkabout on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/walkaboutminigolf/photos/a.123547836545882/130001192567213/?type=3)). Par is set per hole and per course; the only documented totals are Tiki à Coco at **55 regular / 57 hard** and Journey to the Center of the Earth at **56 / 60**, which contradicts an early review's claim that hard mode lowers par ([Tiki à Coco wiki](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Tiki_%C3%A0_Coco); [Journey wiki](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Journey_to_the_Center_of_the_Earth); [CogConnected](https://cogconnected.com/review/walkabout-mini-golf-review/)).

Blow-ups are capped. "Each hole has a set stroke limit, depending on Par count"; with **Stroke Limit** on, "the last allowed stroke is a triple bogey, and missing that stroke counts as a quadruple bogey", so par + 3 strokes are allowed and par + 4 is recorded if you fail; it can be turned off in the Course scorecard's "Course" tab, which "lets you keep hitting the ball without automatic advancement" ([Fandom Game Settings](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Settings); [PSNProfiles](https://psnprofiles.com/guide/16777-walkabout-mini-golf-trophy-guide)). Water and overshoots are not punished beyond the wasted stroke: "hit the ball too hard in a course, get it sunk in water, or overshoot the putting green. This will put the ball 'out of bounds' and it will respawn itself in front of you" ([PSNProfiles](https://psnprofiles.com/guide/16777-walkabout-mini-golf-trophy-guide)). No source mentions a penalty stroke, and a bug report of a ball wedged beside a ramp that "just kept resetting there" implies the respawn point is the last resting lie rather than the tee ([Steam bug report](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/4/3727324132817873071/?l=latam)). A reviewer summed up the intent: players can "take wild shots without resetting your ball and ruining your fun" ([THE VR GRID](https://www.thevrgrid.com/walkabout-mini-golf/)).

Forgiveness became configurable in Oct 2026. Update 6.8 added **Mulligans**: "Session hosts can set a number of mulligans allowed per player before starting the round or mid-round via a modifier on the scorecard", with "a mulligan button ... added to your wristwatch"; a room wanting a clean card "sets it to zero and nothing has changed" ([vr.org](https://vr.org/articles/walkabout-mini-golf-planet-xylodon-mulligans-hand-tracking-2026); [MIXED](https://mixed-news.com/en/walkabout-mini-golf-planet-xylodon-experimental-hand-tracking-mulligans/)). Outside scored rounds, **Practice** mode "has no scoring, and allows users to play holes in any order with an undo option for strokes" (push the stick back/down after a shot) and lets you warp to any hole by clicking its number on the scorecard map ([Mighty Coconut resources](https://www.mightycoconut.com/wmg-resources); [Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2812327682)). No source documents a ball marker, concede, shot clock or in-round replay-a-hole; Race Mode is the only timed mode.

Turn flow is loose by design. The default order is **Honors** (lowest score on the previous hole putts first), with **Distance** and **Simultaneous** as alternatives; Simultaneous "is not available for Match Mode" ([Gaming Age](https://gaming-age.com/2024/06/walkabout-mini-golf-gets-new-game-modes-for-all-courses-and-a-free-remaster/); [Steam announcements](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?feed=steam_community_announcements&enddate=1718293531)). Even under Honors, each player's ball waits at the next tee, so you can "start at the next hole and stand there while the previous person finishes" ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-steam-iphone/)). Whether other players' balls collide with yours is not stated anywhere, a gap that matters for Simultaneous and Race play. Locomotion is layered: "Pressing the trigger moves you to the ball. Pressing the trigger again puts you right behind the ball", **Auto Teleport** does this when the ball stops, the left stick walks and the right turns, snap turning with adjustable angle or smooth turning with adjustable speed, and a **Flyabout** flight mode is reached without the menu by "holding the stick upward for a few seconds until the controller vibrates" ([Fandom Game Settings](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Settings); [Steam comfort guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2661905340); [Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2812327682)). Flight is a convenience, tied to no rule.

Settings live in the Welcome Island Shack and in the scorecard's cog, with a dedicated **Accessibility** tab ([Mighty Coconut support](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/article/17-accessibility-features/)). The named options the notes support:

## Every course ships twice, and the scenery is the progression

Each course is really two: the regular (usually daytime) 18 and a **Hard** (usually night) 18 in which "every hole has been modified, and sometimes completely redesigned to provide an entirely new course", often with cup and tee positions inverted and more obstacles; Holiday Hideaway inverts the convention with a Christmas-morning hard course ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1408230/Walkabout_Mini_Golf_VR/); [Voices of VR #1560](https://voicesofvr.com/1560-walkabout-mini-golfs-incredible-fusion-of-worldbuilding-gameplay-social-dynamics-dlc-experimentation/); [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/holiday-hideaway-tour-walkabout-mini-golf/)). The difficulty jump is real: one reviewer went from **−14 on regular to +12 on hard** of the same course ([Use A Potion](https://www.useapotion.com/2022/01/walkabout-mini-golf-quest-2-review/)). Hard unlocks per course by either route: the studio's own wording is "Collect 10 to unlock Hard Mode!", and reviews add the scoring route, though they disagree whether it is "scoring at or under par" or strictly "below par"; the official par wording was not retrievable ([Walkabout on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/walkaboutminigolf/photos/a.123547836545882/130001192567213/?type=3); [THE VR GRID](https://www.thevrgrid.com/walkabout-mini-golf/); [UploadVR review](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-review/)). The one exception is the Homestar Runner pack's "Bubs' Many Golfs" (Strong Badia), the only course with no Hard version or putter hunt ([Mighty Coconut support](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/article/76-homestar-runner-distraction-pack/)).

The two collectible systems map one-to-one onto the two versions. On the regular course "there are 18 lost balls on every #WalkaboutMiniGolf regular course", one hidden per hole; you collect one by walking up and pulling the thumbstick down, which "vacuums a found ball into your collection", and it becomes an equippable ball in the Pro Shop ([Walkabout on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/walkaboutminigolf/photos/a.123547836545882/130001192567213/?type=3); [UploadVR review](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-review/); [Welcome Island wiki](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Welcome_Island)). On the hard course a **Foxhunt** replaces the balls: "a clue-driven scavenger hunt ... after-which you earn a commemorative putter to use in-game"; you start near hole 1 by picking up the item an arrow points to, the current clue shows on your wristwatch, and the putter is equipped back at the Welcome Shack ([Mighty Coconut glossary](https://www.mightycoconut.com/wmg-glossary); [PSFanatic](https://www.psfanatic.com/walkabout-mini-golf-ps-vr2-tourist-trap-foxhunt-guide-get-a-new-putter/)). Laser Lair's hard course has two hunts and two putters, Myst reportedly three; named rewards include Captain Fawkes' Saber (Tourist Trap) and the Goblin King's clawed putter (Labyrinth) ([Laser Lair wiki](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Laser_Lair); [Labyrinth wiki](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Labyrinth)). Nothing suggests any ball or putter changes physics; they are commemorative, and the collectibles are gated to ownership: a **Guest Pass** lets a non-owner play a friend's DLC course in a private room, "but without the ability for that guest to collect lost balls nor participate in foxhunts" ([Mighty Coconut glossary](https://www.mightycoconut.com/wmg-glossary)). By the Alice course (Dec 2025) the studio counted "1,332 holes to master, over 650 balls to find, 40+ commemorative putters", with roughly 84 million lost balls and 55 million foxhunt clues found by players ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/mighty-coconut-unveils-alices-adventures-in-wonderland-course-for-walkabout-mini-golf/)).

Modifiers were added later and kept out of the record books. Slingshot target areas arrived on some courses (Aug 2024); **Mini Mode** makes players half size and **Giant Mode** enlarges them (first on 28 May 2025 for Cherry Blossom, Sweetopia, Temple at Zerzura and Venice); a **Low/Normal/High gravity** modifier exists on courses such as Bogey's Bonanza, Laser Lair and Venice; and "course records are not saved while modifiers are active" ([Mighty Coconut resources](https://www.mightycoconut.com/wmg-resources); [Giant Mode wiki](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Giant_Mode); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/0/599643705297635607)). The fan wiki's course table tracks exactly these columns per course: Base Course, Sling-shots, Mini Mode, Giant Mode, Gravity and Tasks ([WMG Golf Courses](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Golf_Courses)).

The lobby is itself a place. Since the Apr 2022 update (a 1.6 GB overhaul that "replaced a starting area which had only served as a selection area for balls, clubs and courses"), **Welcome Island** is where "mini golfers can wait for their friends", with a driving range that "keeps score on how many targets are hit", a 38-hole putting green where you can pick up and throw balls, a pool, a raft tour through a hidden cave, and the Shack that "holds every course the player owns and every course available to buy" ([MIXED](https://mixed-news.com/en/walkabout-mini-golf-major-update-brings-driving-range-and-more/); [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-welcome-island/); [Android Central](https://androidcentral.com/gaming/virtual-reality/walkabout-mini-golf-vr-adds-new-social-space-with-driving-range-and-more); [Guild Order, unofficial](https://guildorder.com/games/walkabout/wiki/settings-and-controls-to-teach-a-new-member)). Below the Shack is a hidden developer break room with locked staff doors that "offer secret access to the hard course"; courses are seeded with recurring easter eggs (tiny skulls, bananas, hidden skeletons), and Holiday Hideaway has a hidden hole 18 ([Welcome Island wiki](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Welcome_Island); [WMG Easter Eggs](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Easter_Eggs); [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/holiday-hideaway-tour-walkabout-mini-golf/)). Chess (Welcome Island, Upside Town, Venice), slingshots, leaf blowers and bug nets were layered on over time, but a 2026 studio message says "Employee Mode, Chess and Slingshots are being scaled back" ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-chess/); [Mighty Coconut update](https://www.mightycoconut.com/updates/forgotten-fairyland); [Mighty Coconut blog](https://www.mightycoconut.com/blog/upcoming-changes)).

## 42 courses in six years at $3.99 to $4.99, with no battle pass

The game launched on Quest on 24 Sep 2020 at $14.99 "as a solo project with just 4 courses" and grew to eight free courses (Tourist Trap, Cherry Blossom, Seagull Stacks, Arizona Modern, Original Gothic, Bogey's Bonanza, Tethys Station, Quixote Valley), with Cherry Blossom and Tourist Trap later remastered ([Mighty Coconut](https://www.mightycoconut.com/updates/priceupdate2025); [VR Wiki](https://vr.fandom.com/wiki/Walkabout_Mini_Golf_VR)). From Nov 2021 courses have shipped as individual DLC "roughly every eight weeks", each a "standard 36 holes, 18 Easy and 18 Hard, with associated lost balls and putter" ([Voices of VR #1560](https://voicesofvr.com/1560-walkabout-mini-golfs-incredible-fusion-of-worldbuilding-gameplay-social-dynamics-dlc-experimentation/); [Mighty Coconut blog](https://www.mightycoconut.com/blog/hh-update)). UploadVR called Escape from Planet Xylodon (8 Oct 2026) "the forty-second course" and "the fifth of a planned six new courses for 2026"; the fan wiki counts 43 by including Strong Badia ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-returns-to-space-in-escape-from-planet-xylodon-dlc-course-next-week/); [WMG Golf Courses](https://walkabout-mini-golf.fandom.com/wiki/Golf_Courses)).

## Eight-player private rooms, voice, and deliberately no leaderboards

There are exactly three ways to play: "Play solo, 1v1 online quick match, or create a private room with up to 8 players" ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1408230/Walkabout_Mini_Golf_VR/)). **Quick Match** "will pair you and one other player in a Front or Back 9 of a randomly chosen base game course. These are designed to be rapid-fire games" ([Mighty Coconut support](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/article/12-game-modes/)). A **Private Room** is keyed by a name, not a number: "start a private room, create a name, and share it with friends to join you!", and "the room host (whoever creates the room first) can choose different courses and Game Mode types" ([Mighty Coconut glossary](https://mightycoconut.com/wmg-glossary); [Mighty Coconut support](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/article/12-game-modes/)). Because anyone with the name can walk in, fan guides steer players to platform friend invites instead ([ingamenews, unofficial](https://ingamenews.com/games/walkabout-mini-golf-vr/guides/how-to-host-friends-and-share-dlc-with-guest-pass/)). The cap was 5 until the Labyrinth update of 28 Jul 2022 raised it to 8 ([Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-labyrinth-dlc-release-date-trailer-expanded-multiplayer/); [Mighty Coconut](https://www.mightycoconut.com/labyrinth)). Since 6.8 a host can kick a player and block them from every room that host opens ([vr.org](https://vr.org/articles/walkabout-mini-golf-planet-xylodon-mulligans-hand-tracking-2026)). There is no public room browser; group play with strangers runs through community Discords.

Cross-play covers Quest, PS VR2, Steam/PCVR and Pico, "each crossplayable with up to 8 players together", with no cross-buy between stores; PS VR2 players need PS Plus and can switch cross-play off ([Mighty Coconut blog](https://www.mightycoconut.com/blog/2025start); [Mighty Coconut PSVR2 blog](https://mightycoconut.com/blog/psvr2); [Steam FAQ](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/4/3187986750504779517)). The iPhone Pocket Edition joined that network on 10 Oct 2024 but "crossplay functionality is being removed from Pocket Edition beginning with Blokhaven's release on May 7th, and the App Store listing is being discontinued" ([Mighty Coconut](https://www.mightycoconut.com/blog/ios-sunset)). No Apple Vision Pro version exists ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1531384971)). Quest 1 owners are on a frozen build and "locked to multiplayer with other Quest 1 devices only" since the Meow Wolf update ([Mighty Coconut](https://www.mightycoconut.com/quest1)).

The modes, all host-selectable in a room, differ as follows:

Communication is voice only. Every room has "voice chat no matter what platform they're playing on"; the player list carries an audio icon per name to mute, and players can also mute themselves or others from the scorecard, which doubles as the in-round social panel ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1408230/Walkabout_Mini_Golf_VR/); [Mighty Coconut support](https://support.mightycoconut.com/kb/article/41-microphone-troubleshooting/)). No text chat, emotes, pointers or spatial-audio attenuation appear in any source, and there is no formal spectator role: an observer simply flies or teleports alongside and is told to mute from the scorecard ([Steam FAQ](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/4/3187986750504779517)). Avatars are the studio's own, "featuring lip sync and over a QUADRILLION combinations", extended by free sets and $1.99 licensed packs (Fraggle Rock, Dark Crystal, Exploding Kittens) and by heads bundled with DLC courses; an interviewer's question "Will there ever be full-body avatars?" implies they are head-and-hands, though no source states it ([PlayStation](https://www.playstation.com/en-hk/games/walkabout-mini-golf); [Meta Quest store](https://www.meta.com/experiences/fraggle-rock-avatar-pack/1142044903570614/); [MIXED](https://mixed-news.com/en/walkabout-mini-golf-vr-is-one-of-the-most-successful-vr-games-we-spoke-with-its-creator/)).

Competition is pushed outside the client. "The studio has intentionally not built global leaderboards to emphasize personal enjoyment and individual progression over sheer competition" ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/mighty-coconut-unveils-alices-adventures-in-wonderland-course-for-walkabout-mini-golf/)). Mighty Coconut "does not organize tournaments but is happy to have them hosted on its game": organisers file a Recognized Tournament application, accept its terms, label events "Walkabout Recognized Tournament" and report registration, start and winner ([Mighty Coconut](https://mightycoconut.com/wmg-tournaments)). ## Place first, physics cheated in the player's favour

Martell, an animator who spent six years on the 2009 short "Pigeon: Impossible" and, with much of the team, "ten years working in Hollywood", frames Walkabout as a place before a golf game ([Austin Chronicle](https://www.austinchronicle.com/screens/now-streaming-in-austin-pigeon-impossible-11776486/); [Press Play News](https://pressplaynews.net/2022/04/15/developer-interview-walkabout-mini-golf-meets-myst/)). "Half the fun of mini golf lies in the course rather than the game"; he thinks of the course as the centrepiece, and the "Walkabout Path" came from planning each course "the way you would storyboard a film" ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/principles-of-course-design-in-walkabout-mini-golf/)). The 18-hole format "kind of provides that structure" for worldbuilding-first design, and the game's relevance "comes from the world building and the care", each course feeling like a different world "despite the same core mechanic" ([Voices of VR #1560](https://voicesofvr.com/1560-walkabout-mini-golfs-incredible-fusion-of-worldbuilding-gameplay-social-dynamics-dlc-experimentation/); [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-creator-lucas-martell-sandbox/)). The studio's physics rule is explicit: "it's okay to cheat the physics if it helps the player" ([Voices of VR #1560](https://voicesofvr.com/1560-walkabout-mini-golfs-incredible-fusion-of-worldbuilding-gameplay-social-dynamics-dlc-experimentation/)). That rule plus the course-as-centrepiece stance explains the feel described above: believable, forgiving putting inside a scene worth walking through, not a simulator.

The game began on phones, "a game for iPhone and Android where you swing your phone like a putter", and multiplayer was not in the plan: "Oculus [now Meta] actually, they pushed us to add multiplayer in there"; Martell coded "a crude multiplayer version" and realised "he needed someone to play with" ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-steam-iphone/); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/the-best-quest-2-mini-golf-game-showed-me-that-the-metaverse-could-be-amazing); [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-creator-lucas-martell-sandbox/)). He now holds that "sense of space and your surroundings is critical in multiplayer VR" and that "players use Walkabout Mini Golf as a place to meet with people they know or others who share interests" ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/the-best-quest-2-mini-golf-game-showed-me-that-the-metaverse-could-be-amazing); [MIXED](https://mixed-news.com/en/walkabout-mini-golf-vr-is-one-of-the-most-successful-vr-games-we-spoke-with-its-creator/)). The social design grew out of the walkabout structure rather than being planned: standing around while friends putt "is part of the appeal", and only the putter is virtual, so "little has been abstracted away" ([A Damn Fine Cup of Culture](https://adamnfinecup.com/2025/01/25/they-create-worlds-walkabout-mini-golf/)).

The course pipeline is a film pipeline. Work starts "mostly conceptual": inspiration searches and mood boards, then pencil sketches, then a to-scale blockout in Gravity Sketch, "meeting in VR to build to scale". "Placeholders exist for all 18 holes, and the room is shaped first, with furniture moved so each hole fits"; a Design Intent Document "sets goals while leaving room for change", and the model passes "through lighting, sound, animation, and gameplay teams over roughly 15 to 18 months" ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/principles-of-course-design-in-walkabout-mini-golf/); [Mighty Coconut on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wjv7K1HNdiY)). Pacing is staged: "Each course has a main walkabout route with reveals along the way, and designers add a midpoint highlight at the ninth hole", and "Hard versions often include a few holes that are almost entirely new designs" ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/principles-of-course-design-in-walkabout-mini-golf/)). The studio "builds worlds differently from most game companies, where artists typically make individual pieces", treating theme-park environmental storytelling as central; Hollywood is "structured like a three-act story", and for Wonderland Martell pushed the team to ask "what the course's core concept is and what it would inspire in players" ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/how-lucas-martell-brings-hollywood-production-storytelling-to-walkabout-mini-golf/); [Press Play News](https://pressplaynews.net/2025/11/06/developer-interview-alices-adventures-in-wonderland-walkabout-mini-golf/)). Lost balls and Foxhunts are how the studio reconciles "scenery first" with "give the player something to do": looking becomes playing. The mulligan addition drew the only philosophical pushback found, a columnist unsure a do-over belongs in a game built on consequence ([VR.org](https://vr.org/articles/walkabout-mini-golf-planet-xylodon-mulligans-hand-tracking-2026)).

## Platforms, numbers, reception and a 2026 contraction

The platform path runs Quest (24 Sep 2020, "just weeks before the Quest 2"), Steam with cross-play (15 Jul 2021), Pico (2022 lineup), Viveport, PS VR2 (11 May 2023, all DLC at launch), iOS Pocket Edition (10 Oct 2024, $4.99, DLC $1.99; retired May 2026) ([VR & AR Wiki](https://vrarwiki.com/wiki/Walkabout_Mini_Golf); [Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/vr-walkabout-mini-golf-steam-release-date-cross-play/); [UploadVR](https://uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-coming-psvr-2-release-date); [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-iphone-price-release-date/)). Performance targets are modest and steady: the Meta listing promises "90hz on Quest 2, 3, and Pro", and the Dec 2023 Quest 3 update raised render scale from 1.33 to 1.5, "the same resolution scale as PCVR and PSVR2", with 90 FPS up from 80 on Quest 2 ([Meta Quest store](https://www.meta.com/experiences/walkabout-mini-golf/2462678267173943/); [Mighty Coconut blog](https://www.mightycoconut.com/blog/quest3)). There is no passthrough or mixed-reality mode ([Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/house-of-golf-vr-quest-release-date-trailer)). The business is self-funded: "everything you see in Walkabout has been funded 100% by people purchasing the game, courses and growing our community", and the team grew "from just me at the very beginning, to 3, to 8, then 12 ... to our biggest point where we had 36 people" ([Mighty Coconut blog](https://www.mightycoconut.com/blog/5-years-numbers); [Mighty Coconut blog](https://www.mightycoconut.com/blog/mc-update-follow-up)).

Usage figures at five years (Sep 2025): **23,004,672 hours played, 43,365,815 sessions, more than two billion putts**, players in over 40 countries, "20th best-selling title of all time for Meta Quest", and "over 55% of VR sessions being multiplayer" ([Mighty Coconut blog](https://www.mightycoconut.com/blog/5-years-numbers); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/mighty-coconut-unveils-alices-adventures-in-wonderland-course-for-walkabout-mini-golf/)). Critically, OpenCritic shows a **Top Critic Average of 88 with 100% recommending**, UploadVR gave its top "Essential" label, Steam sits at "Overwhelmingly Positive" (97.6% of ~3.1k reviews; 98% of 3,348 in Jul 2026) and the Quest store at **4.8/5 over 13,448 ratings** ([OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/10414/walkabout-mini-golf); [UploadVR review](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-review/); [steamdata.ai](https://steamdata.ai/game/1408230); [queststoredb](https://queststoredb.com/game/walkabout-mini-golf/2462678267173943)). It won UploadVR's Best Updated VR Game 2024 and Best Virtual Place 2025, a category name that itself captures how the game is read ([UploadVR 2024 awards](https://www.uploadvr.com/uploadvr-best-of-vr-2024-awards/); [UploadVR 2025 awards](https://www.uploadvr.com/best-of-vr-2025-awards/)). Criticism is mild and consistent: "little in the way of help or guidance", per-course DLC that rarely goes on sale, occasional physics oddities, and early hard modes that "simply remix[ed] previous holes" ([VR Lowdown](https://vrlowdown.com/best-vr-golf-games/); [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1408230/discussions/0/597387624656733143/); [Checkpoint Gaming](https://checkpointgaming.net/features/2021/02/vr-corner-walkabout-mini-golf/)). Against rivals, reviewers rank Walkabout first for fun and physics-based challenge, Cloudlands VR Minigolf second (with a course editor Walkabout lacks), and Golf+/Pro Putt as the pick for real putting practice ([VR Lowdown](https://vrlowdown.com/best-vr-golf-games/)).

The model proved sensitive to the VR market's new-buyer inflow. On 23 Jan 2026 the studio cut about 25% (around 8 jobs, 27 remaining) alongside the $4.99 DLC price; on 28 Sep 2026 it announced 10 more departures (about 35%) toward a team of 17, with Martell saying he had "failed to anticipate how rapidly and deeply the trajectory would continue", that it "may lead to a slower cadence of DLC course releases", and that "new games are the best way to diversify revenue and stabilize things" ([Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-lays-off-2026/); [Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/walkabout-mini-golf-studio-announces-another-round-of-lay-offs-following-dlc-price-hike/); [Mighty Coconut blog](https://www.mightycoconut.com/blog/mc-update-follow-up)). The first such game is Walkabout Dungeon Golfer, teased as an April Fools joke and formally announced on 10 Aug 2026 as a roguelike dungeon crawler for VR and flatscreen with cross-play, aimed at summer 2027 ([UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/walkabout-dungeon-golfer-officially-in-production-after-fake-april-fools-reveal/); [Mighty Coconut blog](https://www.mightycoconut.com/blog/mcpresents)).

## Conclusion

For a developer building a 3D multiplayer web game, Walkabout's most transferable lesson is structural rather than mechanical: one physical verb everyone already knows, a rule set whose only cost is the stroke you already took, and a world laid out as a walk whose hidden goals turn looking into play. The two unlock routes (10 balls or par) let explorers and golfers reach the same Hard content, and gating collectibles rather than access behind ownership (Guest Pass) keeps rooms full without giving the content away. Small named rooms with voice, a host who owns the settings, an "observer" who is simply a non-playing body in the space, and no leaderboards together create a hangout where competition is organised by the community rather than the client. The settings surface is a checklist of the problems a swing-based game must solve: false hits (Grip To Putt, look gating), power calibration (Strength), seated play (Lock Ball), and blow-ups (Stroke Limit, Mulligans).

Two things the record does not supply are exactly the two a web port would need: the netcode (authority, ball synchronisation, whether balls collide) and the swing-to-ball algorithm. Both must be designed rather than copied, and the studio's own rule, cheat the physics when it helps the player, is the best available guidance for both. The 2026 contraction also shows the ceiling of the model: a steady cadence of small paid worlds sustains a studio only while new players keep arriving, which is why the next Walkabout is a different game on flat screens as well as in headsets.
