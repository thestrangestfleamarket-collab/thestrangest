# A week of 30 players (simulated 9 Nov 2026)

How: `tools/weeksim.cjs` (20 seeded weeks, the game's own rates and the server's bucket) and `tools/loadsim.cjs` (30 clients on the real `Block` under `wrangler dev`). The server holes were confirmed live, not just read. Nothing in the game was changed.

## The week in numbers (average of 20 runs)
- 20 players at launch and 10 more through the week; 17 active on day 7; at most about 7 online at once (Central evenings).
- Day 7 wealth: median $1,750, top 10% $24,300, richest $38,400 (Gini 0.67).
- Earned $210k, spent $14k: 93% of the money stays in the game.
- Income: dungeon 46%, busking 25%, foraging (fairy rings) 21%, fishing 4%, everything else 4%.
- Dollars an hour: fighter $2,900, grinder $2,300, musician $1,000, explorer $355, fisher $283, decorator $237, casual $87, social $58.
- The server's bucket cut $33k of earnings, all from grinders and fighters.
- Fairy rings and busking were found by day 1–2 and spread through chat to every optimiser.

## P1: broken now
1. **The starter kit loses 798 units on a new player's first save.** The kit is 1,404 units and the item bucket holds 600. Lost: all INVISIBILITY, STRENGTH, SPEED, KEEN EYE, HEALING, JUMP, LUCK and SHRINK, 11 FLIGHT, and the skateboard. The client then deletes them (`OWNED.onClip`).
2. **A stall makes items from nothing.** `put` 9999 of anything you don't own, then `take`: the server hands back 9999 and allows them in the bucket.
3. **A stall buy never checks the buyer's cash.** A $1,000,000 buy by a player holding $1,000 went through; the seller collected $950,000. The seller can also raise the price after the buyer opens the window (`put` n=0 resets the price), so an unmodified buyer can lose everything they carry.
4. **A trade hands over what the payer claims.** A player with $0 "paid" $2,000,000 and 5,000 meteorites; the other side kept them.

Items 2–4 bypass the whole "money on the server" fix. Every one needs only the browser console.

## P2: economy and fun
5. **No money sinks.** All live tools together cost about $3,400, and the kit already covers most of them. Hunger is off, healing is free, and nothing wears out. There's no rent, tax or upkeep. After the first day or two the only things left to buy are furniture, pets and the fish tank.
6. **The starter kit empties the shops.** The best board, the BMX, the kart, the camera and 99 of every power mushroom cost $0. Power mushrooms aren't sold anywhere, so they're a buff that never comes back.
7. **Loops with no cap (only the $40-a-minute bucket stops them):**
   - Busking: about $400 an hour at the start, $3,400 at guitar 50, $8,400 at guitar 100.
   - Fairy rings: 3–4 morels or moonglows about every 2 minutes, with no skill check.
   - Paintings: $1 paper sells for $13.
   - Garden: a $2 seed grows a $24 flower, even at zero skill.
   - Free film for the camera; butterflies come back when a chunk reloads; bounties repeat with no wait.
   - A grinder hits the cap. A fisher earns a tenth of a busker.
8. **Fishing, bugs, birds and photos pay too little to matter** next to the loops (5% of income together), even though they're the game's cosiest things to do.
9. **The arcade trains skills for free to 40.0, while lessons cost money and stop at 30.0.**
10. **5 stalls for the whole section, kept forever by a $15 top-up each day.** In the test, 5 of 30 players got one. Only other players buy, and a new player already owns most things, so demand is thin.
11. **Social players earn nearly nothing** (board games, chat, the bar: $58 an hour), so they fall far behind.
12. **The Mushroom Seller's own window buys anything sellable, archived items too.** This goes against the 9 Nov "mushrooms only" decision.

## P2: multiplayer
13. **PvP works everywhere, apartments included.** There are no safe zones and no new-player protection. Damage is judged by the attacker, so a modified client knocks someone out in about 0.3 s (20 hits at 0.5 landed in the test). An unmodified ganker can faint someone and then kill them, and the body's loot goes to anyone after an hour. In the model about 1.7 players a week lose a body.
14. **Any client can rewrite shared state:** the dungeon's monster grid (`mobgrid`), the folk walk grid and cast, and 6,000 `tag:` keys of 4 KB each.
15. **Daily finds reset on the device's clock**, so moving the PC clock forward re-arms them.
16. **Flats 17 and up all land on floor 9** (14 of 30 players), because the tower clamps to its top floor.

## Load: fine
- 30 players on one section: each client gets 182 messages a second (13 KB/s), the server sends 5,450 a second, and a round trip is 5 ms median, 51 ms p95.
- Movement goes to everyone in the section regardless of distance or room (n², no interest management). That's fine at 30, but needs a radius before hundreds.
- Estimated cost: a few dollars a week.

## Fix order suggested (each needs the owner)
1. The server checks the pack before `put`, `buy` and `paid` (its `/owned` copy), and charges the stall rent itself.
2. The kit is allowed in the bucket (`econAllow`, like the owners' kit), or the bucket is raised for a first save.
3. A sink, the owner's pick: things wear (reverses 5 Nov), rent on the flat, or consumable power mushrooms sold again.
4. Bring the loops into line: a cap on busking tips per hour, fairy rings needing myco skill, a minimum price for paintings.
5. PvP: no fights in apartments, and the bath and market as safe zones.
