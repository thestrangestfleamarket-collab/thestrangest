# YATAI '86 credits

- People and their motion (`data/sushi/avatars/`, `data/sushi/anim/`): the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library, https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox, MIT (`docs/licences/rocketbox-mit.txt`). The rigs are as exported; their pictures were made 1024 px WebP; the motion (walk_neutral_01, sit_table_idle_neutral_01, sit_table_idle_look_around, sit_table_idle_waiting_01, the men's and the women's) was baked to rotations and the hips' height.
- Everything else (the cart, the street, the food, the pictures, the sounds) is made in the page's own code.
