# RAMEN credits

- People and their motion (`data/sushi/cast.json`, from `node tools/sushi-cast.cjs`): Polyfork, https://polyfork.dev, its licence (use in games, no reselling the files). The villagers are its Japanese set (farmer, townswoman, monk, samurai, foot soldier) as they are, and a few of its other bodies (the fisherman, a villager, a child, a grandmother, a frontier townswoman, a farmer, a gardener, the chef) with their clothes dyed to period colours in the page (`DRESS` in `src/sushi/pf.js`).
- The goldfish is Polyfork's koi (`data/sushi/koi.json`), gilded in the page.
- Everything else (the stall, the machiya, the shrine gate, the well, the lanterns, the braziers, the coins, the food) is made in the page's own code, but for what is listed below.

- RAMEN's night (`tools/ramen-arms.py`): Poly Haven, CC0: the katana (`antique_katana_01`), the woodcutter's axe (`wooden_axe`), the barricade's crates (`wooden_crate_01`) and barrel (`wine_barrel_01`). The other period arms (cleaver, sickle, tanto, wakizashi, naginata, yari, bamboo spear, staff, kunai, shuriken, yumi and arrows, tanegashima, kusarigama), the paper lantern, the brazier and the fire-watch tower are made in `src/sushi/weapons.js` and `arsenal.js` from the photographed woods and steel.
- The recorded sounds (`data/sushi/snd/`): OpenGameArt, CC0, listed in `docs/sushi-sounds.md`.
- Everything else (the cart's and the street's shapes, the food, the signs, the other sounds) is made in the page's own code.
