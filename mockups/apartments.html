APARTMENTS REDESIGN

Mockup for the owner, 10 Nov 2026. Nothing is built. Four apartment houses, each its own brick and its own size of flat. The front door takes you straight into your own flat; the callbox beside it rings a neighbour. No lobby, lift or halls.

1 · WHERE THEY STAND

West St's road was taken out this morning ("No roads in park at all"; West St is park now), so "down West St" needs a choice. Both options keep Market St as it is east of the Tower.

A · A ROW ALONG THE PARK'S WEST EDGE

The four fronts stand on the section's west line where West St ran, facing the park across a footpath (no road, no cars). The Tower's lot on Market St becomes a small plaza or one more shop.

B · WEST ST COMES BACK AS A SHORT STREET

West St's road returns from Market St north for four lots, with its pavement and parked cars; the park keeps everything east of it. Reverses "West St is park" for that stretch.

2 · THE FOUR HOUSES

Each is one painted block like every building (door, callbox, windows, fire escape or stoop); only the door and the callbox are used.

3 · THE FOUR FLATS

5 · WHAT CHANGES

  • Goes: THE TOWER, its lobby, lift, stairs and halls; the map's jump to any tenant's flat and VISIT on a player's card (the callbox is the way in), unless you want those kept.
  • Stays: the flat itself, DECORATE, your fire escape, starting at home, pets and the tank at home, a visitor seeing your layout.
  • New: four houses, four sizes of flat, the callbox, the buzzer.

YOUR PICKS

COST

  • One flat is built at a time, as today; the four houses are four painted blocks, cheaper than the Tower's ten storeys.
