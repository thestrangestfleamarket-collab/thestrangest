THE AQUARIUM

Mockup. Sea life gets its own shop, next to the pet store's idea: every fish and sea animal in the game in its own lit tank, pet-sized. Buy one and it goes to the big fish tank in your apartment (every apartment has one), where it swims with the others and eats when you feed the tank. Crabs stay at the pet store (they walk with you).

FROM THE DOOR

EVERY TANK

HOW IT PLAYS

  • Double-click a tank (or E): buy it. It waits in your apartment's tank; PETS lists it (name, age, hunger, loyalty).
  • Fish food (bodega, pet store) feeds the whole tank.
  • Each tank stays full: everyone can buy the same one. The middle tank is to look at: a few of each swim together.
