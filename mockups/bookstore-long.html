Plan

Back wall

  • One long wall of books facing the windows: you see the whole collection from the door, and from the street through the glass.
  • Every case is at floor height, so you walk up and double-click any of them.
  • The room is shallow, so the reading corner (rug, armchair, two tub chairs, lamps) sits in the middle with the counter by the door.

Back wall

  • A 7.4 m back wall only fits 8 cases across, so the 16 are stacked in two tiers, using the taller ceiling, with a brass rail and a rolling ladder.
