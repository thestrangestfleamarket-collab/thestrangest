IDEAS FROM OTHER GAMES

Starting over. For each thing we still need, how games close to ours handle it: life and town games (Animal Crossing, Stardew Valley, The Sims), online worlds (Ultima Online, RuneScape, World of Warcraft) and open cities (GTA, Red Dead Redemption 2), plus Fortnite. Under each is the version that fits a market full of strange things, many players, skills, shops, pets and apartments. OUR TAKE is my pick for each; the drawings are ours, only the ideas are borrowed.

WHAT WE NEED: 1 the ESC hub (menus and things) · 2 holding and carrying things · 3 five rarities · 4 the item card · 5 picking up and the feed · 6 emotes · 7 what a flea market adds (appraising, condition).

1 · THE HUB ESC: getting to every menu and every thing

THE PIE MENUThe Sims · Second LifeClick a thing or a person: a ring of what you can do with it opens right there (SIT, BUY, TALK, PICK UP). Nothing to remember.
THE WEAPON WHEELGTA · Red Dead Redemption 2Hold a key: a wheel of kinds of things in the middle of the screen. Point and let go. Red Dead also has a second wheel for the satchel (food, tonics, tools).
THE PHONE OF APPSAnimal Crossing (NookPhone) · GTA (the phone)A phone in your hand with a grid of apps: MAP, CAMERA, PASSPORT, RECIPES, CALL A CAB. Every menu is an app; new apps arrive as you play.
THE TOOL RINGAnimal CrossingUp on the d-pad: a ring of only your tools (net, rod, shovel, watering can). Quick; the bag stays its own page.
OUR TAKE · THE WHEEL WITH TWO SIDES

ESC opens one wheel in the middle of the screen, Red Dead's two wheels made one. The top half is the menus (SETTINGS, WHAT YOU CAN DO, PETS, MAP, EMOTES, ADMIN for owners); the bottom half is your things by kind (TOOLS, FOOD, MUSIC, RIDES, LIGHTS, WEAR, FINDS, OTHER). A menu slice opens its window as today; a kind slice puts its first thing in your hands, the mouse wheel steps to the next. The middle is the BAG (the whole list). The game doesn't pause: other players are there.

Also worth taking later: the Sims' pie menu for things in the world (in place of double-click's guess). Not part of this.

OUR TAKE · POCKETS THAT GROW, BY KIND

Today the bag has no limit. Take Stardew's growing pack: the bag holds 24 kinds (a stack is one), and the bodega sells a bigger pack (36, then 48). The quick bar stays the hotbar. What doesn't fit goes to your apartment, the bank or the vault. It gives the shops something to sell and makes carrying a choice. The server keeps the count, as it keeps the bag now.

Not weight: we'd need a weight on every thing first.

OUR TAKE · SMALL THINGS BY WALKING, RARE THINGS HELD UP

Small finds (mushrooms, flowers, coins, feathers, shells, raw stones) go in as you walk over them and slide into a feed at the top right in their colour, ×count. A thing someone else dropped, anything big, and STRANGER and THE STRANGEST still need E, and a STRANGER or THE STRANGEST find is held up over your head for a breath, with its name, for every player near to see (Animal Crossing's moment, shared). Drops glow their colour; the top two send up a beam (drawn only near you). A setting turns walking-over off.

H and its squares stay (decided 7 Nov). EMOTES is a slice of the hub; holding H there shows your 8 favourites. Typing /wave in chat plays it, aimed at whoever you face. New emotes come from the folk: each character teaches one when you know them well enough (the Junk Dealer HAGGLE, the Courier a WHEELIE WAVE), so emotes become something to collect.
