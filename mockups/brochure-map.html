THE MARKET'S VISITOR'S MAP · a 1980s brochure

A folded tourist map of THE MARKET, the kind a hotel lobby rack held in 1986. MAP on the pager (or M, or a click on the minimap) brings it up folded, it unfolds across the screen, a click on any place goes there, ESC folds it away.
