WHAT IS DOWN THERE

A pirate ship 48 m long lies broken-backed on the deepest bed of the Hudson, its black flag still on the mast. Swim in through the broken middle: a stern cabin, a hold of barrels and crates.

THE CHESTS

Four in the wreck, three deeper out in the channel. Each gives old coins and a gem, once a day.

THE SHARKS

Four circle the ship. Get close and one comes for you, bites and turns away.

HOW TO DIVE

  1. 1Rent a SCUBA SET at FISHING & SCUBA. One fill is 5 minutes of air.
  2. 2Buy GOGGLES to see under water and a HEADLIGHT: it is dark at the wreck.
  3. 3Walk off the quay into the Hudson.
  4. 4Hold SPACE to swim down, SHIFT to come up. Through your own eyes, swim where you look.
  5. 5About 25 seconds down you reach the ship.
  6. 6Double-click a chest to open it.
