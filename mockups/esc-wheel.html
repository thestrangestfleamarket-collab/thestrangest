ESC · THE WHEEL

ESC opens a wheel round the middle of the screen. Everything is in it: SETTINGS, WHAT YOU CAN DO, PETS, the BAG and its tabs, MAP, EMOTES and your things by kind; ADMIN and TENANTS too for owners. A slice that is a menu opens that same window as today. A slice that is a kind of thing puts it in your hands. ESC again, or a click off the wheel, closes it. The game does not pause or slow (other players are there); the screen only dims behind the wheel. The quick bar, I, Shift+M, ? and H still open their own pages straight away. The stick does the same on a controller, and so does pointing in VR.

A · TWO RINGSThe inner ring is the menus, the outer ring is your things by kind. Everything is one point away. Point at a kind and the mouse wheel steps through what you have of it (its name in the middle); click holds it.Try it: hover either ring, use the mouse wheel on an outer slice, click.

B · ONE RING, THINGS ONE STEP INOne ring of 8: the menus plus a THINGS slice. Clicking THINGS turns the ring into the kinds (TOOLS, FOOD, MUSIC, RIDES…); right-click or the middle goes back. Bigger, easier slices; things take one click more.Try it: click THINGS, then the middle to go back.

C · THINGS ON THE WHEEL, MENUS UNDER ITThe wheel is only your things by kind (like 2B). The menus are a row of buttons under it with their keys. Closest to GTA; picking a thing is fastest, menus are a click on the row.Try it: hover the wheel, click a button in the row.
