What is wrong (the red numbers)

  1. A kitchen at the back (fridge, sink, stove, a stool, a tea tray). You had kitchens taken out of the apartments on 9 Nov ("remove kitchen items from apartments"); the 14 Nov layout put one back without asking. The stool stands at the end of the stove facing the wall, at no counter.
  2. The night-painting poster hangs behind the sink. Its bottom edge is 1.14 m up, below the counter top (1.42 m): half of it is behind the counter.
  3. Two pictures stick out of the wall. The portrait in the red frame (downstairs) and the koi poster (upstairs) are turned edge-on: each stands out 0.6 m from the east wall, half through it.
  4. The low strip under the upper floor is three unrelated corners. Along the east wall, under the 4.4 m ceiling: the PC desk at the front, the fish tank nook in the middle and the radio's listening chair at the back, each squeezed in.
  5. The dining table stands alone in the middle of the floor. Between the sofa and your door, with the herbs on it; with no kitchen it reads as a table dropped in the walkway.

Proposed

  1. Kitchen out again, as you decided on 9 Nov (fridge, sink, stove, stool, tea tray); the basil goes on the dining table.
  2. The dining table and its four chairs move to the back left, by the windows and the foot of the landing: a dining corner out of the walkway.
  3. The middle of the floor is left open: a clear walk from your door to the living room.
  4. The two pictures lie flat on the wall; the night painting hangs where the kitchen was, at eye height, seen whole.
  5. The radio and its listening chair move a little out from the wall into one corner with the lamp, so the strip under the upper floor reads as study / aquarium / listening, front to back, each with room round it.
  6. Upstairs, the globe table stands beside the floor globe at the front by the telescope, with the moon and Mars globes on it: a stargazer's corner.
  7. Unchanged: the living room before the TV, the fish tank nook, the library, the bed and wardrobe, the plants by the windows and the door.
