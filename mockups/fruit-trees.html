THE REAL RULES (EACH ONE CAN BE ON OR OFF)

POLLINATION

Apples, sweet cherries, pears, pecans, chestnuts, hazels and pawpaws need a second tree of another kind nearby. Pistachio, date, papaya, ginkgo and American persimmon need a male and a female. Bees and wind carry the pollen; pawpaws use flies.

PIPS DON'T COME TRUE

A seed from an apple, pear, avocado or mango grows a random new tree that may fruit badly, or only after ten years. A named kind is a grafted sapling (bought). That makes every pip a lottery, and a good one is worth grafting.

FROST AND WINTER

Zone 7 or colder trees live outside. A fig makes it through winter wrapped in burlap with a bucket on top, as Brooklyn's old Italian gardens do. Citrus and tropicals come in, or die on the first hard frost. A late frost on early blossom (apricot, almond) loses that year's crop.

BIRDS, SQUIRRELS, WASPS

Birds take ripe cherries and serviceberries, and the park's squirrels take nuts. Fruit left on the ground rots and brings wasps. A net or a quick pick saves it.

THINNING AND PRUNING

Leave every peach on and the branch snaps. You thin to one per hand-width. Prune in winter while the tree is bare, and you get a bigger crop next year.

NEIGHBOURS

Black walnut roots poison tomatoes and most plants under them (juglone). Linden and fruit blossom feed bees. A tree shades the plot beside it.

WHAT YOU DO WITH IT

NOW (SYSTEMS THAT EXIST)

  • Eat it: hunger and health (VIT).
  • Sell it: the Tomato's shop already buys what pots grow.
  • Give or trade it to other players.
  • Plant its pit or nut.
  • Climb the tree for the top fruit (tree climbing exists).
  • Cut a dead tree for firewood (the fire).

NEEDS A PLACE (THE KITCHEN IS ARCHIVED)

  • A cider press: apple and pear juice, cider, perry.
  • An olive press: oil.
  • A sugar shack: boil maple and birch sap to syrup (40 to 1, 100 to 1).
  • Drying racks: prunes, dried apricots, figs, dates.
  • Cacao and coffee: ferment, dry and roast the beans.

COST

  • One tree is about 300–600 triangles, and its fruit is one instanced mesh. Far trees use the park's tree cards.
  • Only trees near you are drawn.
  • Each tree is one small server row (kind, owner, spot, planted, last watered, last picked), so everyone sees the same tree.
