EMOTES · INVENTORY · ITEMS — OTHER WAYS

Common ways games like Fortnite and GTA handle these, in our green. Today: emotes are the H window of squares plus the quick bar; the bag is INVENTORY > BAG in the ESC menu (double-click uses, drag to drop, pin or trade). The letters are the pick. Several can mix, for example 1A with 2B.

1 · EMOTES

1A · THE EMOTE WHEEL (Fortnite)Hold H: a wheel of 8 opens round the middle of the screen. Point at one and let go to play it. The wheel's pages switch with the wheel or 1–6. You pick which emotes go on each page in the H window (so the squares stay as the shelf you pick from). Works the same with a controller stick and in VR.Try it: hover a slice, click to play it. The tabs are the pages.

1B · THE INTERACTION MENU (GTA Online)Tap M: a short list in the top left, moved with the arrows or the wheel, while you keep walking. Emotes are one entry among others (STYLE, WALK STYLE, MOOD, YOUR PET, INVENTORY, PASSPORT). The last one you played is kept, so a hold of a key replays it.Try it: click a row.

1C · THE QUICK BAR'S EMOTE ROWKeep the H window and the quick bar as they are; add a second, thin row above the quick bar that shows only while you hold Alt: ten emotes on Alt+1 … Alt+0. Nothing else changes.

2 · INVENTORY

2A · FIVE SLOTS AND A BAG (Fortnite)A short bar of 5 things in your hands (1–5, the wheel scrolls them), each edged in its rarity. Anything else goes in the bag. Carrying is limited, so what you choose to carry matters. The quick bar would stay for menus and emotes only.Try it: click a slot or press 1–5 here.
BAG 14 / 24
2B · THE ITEM WHEEL (GTA)Hold Tab: the world slows (only for you, others see you reach into your bag), and a wheel of kinds opens: TOOLS, FOOD, MUSIC, RIDES, LIGHTS, WEAPONS, PETS, OTHER. Point at a kind to take its first thing; the wheel or Q/E steps through the others in that kind. Let go to hold it.Try it: hover a kind, then the wheel steps through it.

HOLD TAB · POINT · WHEEL STEPS · RELEASE
2C · A GRID WITH SIZES (survival games)The bag is a grid; big things take more squares (the guitar 1×3, the saddle 2×2, a mushroom 1×1). The bag's size is the limit, not a count. Drag to move, R turns a thing while it's held. A bigger bag (a backpack) is more squares. Grid inventories like this (Resident Evil, Tarkov) are the opposite of Fortnite's 5 slots.Try it: hover a thing for its card.
BAG · CANVAS PACK 8×6
2D · THE LOCKER AND THE BODY (Fortnite's locker, GTA's wardrobe)What you wear is its own screen: HEAD, FACE, BODY, LEGS, FEET, BACK, HANDS, a PET that follows, a RIDE. Each slot opens its own list. You save looks as presets (1–5) and swap them in one press. It sits next to the paper doll you get from double-clicking yourself; the bag keeps only what you carry.

3 · ITEMS

3A · RARITY COLOURS EVERYWHERE (Fortnite)Our three tiers get a colour each, used the same everywhere: the bag's edge, the quick bar, the pickup line, the beam over a dropped thing, the trade window. COMMON green, RARE blue, SUPER RARE gold. A dropped thing glows its colour, and a super-rare one sends up a beam you can see from down the street.

3B · THE ITEM CARD (both)Hover anything in the bag, the quick bar, a shop or a trade: one card, the same everywhere. Its 3D model turning, its name, tier, what it does, its numbers (worth, weight, uses left), what skill it feeds, and the keys for it. It would replace the plain line under the bag.
3C · PICKUP AND THE FEED (GTA, Fortnite)Walking over small things picks them up by themselves (mushrooms, coins, feathers); big or owned things still need E. Each find slides into a short feed at the top right (a picture, the name, ×count, its colour) and fades in 4 seconds, in place of the log line.
