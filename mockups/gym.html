Knuckle & Iron

A gym you go into from the street. Every machine trains a stat you already have (STRENGTH, STAMINA, MARTIAL ARTS), and the ring in the middle is a real physics fight: two jointed bodies that wobble, block with their gloves, get knocked down and thrown over the ropes.

The floor

Drag to look around · scroll or pinch to zoom · pick a station to go to it. The runner and the bag boxer are on the same physics bodies as the ring.

The ring: try it

Two bodies of 15 joined parts each. Your moves only pull the parts toward a pose, so a punch can be blocked by a glove, a hit shoves the whole body, and the looser the body the more it flops. Both of you get looser as you get hurt.

How should a fight feel?

The three buttons above are these three. They are one setting (how loose the body is), so it can also be a choice per ring, or per fight.

A

Boxing

  • Tight stance, sharp jab / cross / hook / uppercut
  • Footwork matters; gloves block by being in the way
  • Knockdowns only from a clean head shot or a run of hits
  • No grabs; clinch breaks you apart
RecommendedB

Hybrid

  • Starts as boxing, gets wobblier the more you're hurt
  • Grab and throw allowed: over the top rope is a ring out
  • A knockdown is a real ragdoll fall, then you climb back up
  • Reads as a boxing match until it turns into a brawl
C

Gang brawl

  • Floppy from the first bell, arms swing loose
  • Grabbing, dragging, tossing is most of the fight
  • Funniest with 3–4 players in the ring at once
  • Punch technique matters less than position
