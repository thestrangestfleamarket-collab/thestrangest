GRASS MADE LIKE THE TREES

The oaks' crowns are low-poly leaf clumps: faceted shapes, the leaf mottle in the world (11 cm clumps, bright tips), each clump lit as one round shape, cut into 4 flat bands, cool in the shade, swaying. Three ways to make the lawn from the same stuff, with the current card tufts to compare. Drag to turn the view.
