HOW THE MENUS COULD LOOK: EIGHT WAYS

The same menu in each (the character page with a couple of setting rows), the same HUD, the same talk bar; only the look changes. Every look is flat, grainy and pixel (owner, 2 Nov 2026: "flatter, grainier, pixelated looks, no thin lines"): pixel fonts, 4px block borders, no gradients or glows, flat offset shadows, striped rows instead of rules, a chunky grain over the screen and a 2px dither on the panels. Structure is not decided here (A–D are in mockups/windows.html). Fonts come from Google Fonts: open this in a browser to see them properly.
