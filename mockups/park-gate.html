CENTRAL PARK'S GATE

Three ways to build a bigger entrance with a dark iron gate, the same fence running all the way around the park in place of the brick wall. The mushroom seller and the tomato have their stalls on either side of the entrance, and both are 15% bigger. Drag a picture to turn it.

The stalls are the ones already in the game (three tables in a U, stepped and heaped, the overflow around them), each on its own side of the entrance with its front to the street. Both sellers are 15% bigger (the mushroom seller 2.5 m, the tomato a little over 1.5 m); the stalls stay their size. Drawn the game's way, with flat bands of light, hard shadows and chunky pixels. Pick one, or mix them (for example A's railing with C's three gates).
