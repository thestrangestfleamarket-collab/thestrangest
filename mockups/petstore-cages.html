HOW IT PLAYS

  • Each cage: the animal pet-sized, idling and turning; its name and price on a tag.
  • Double-click a cage (or E): buy it; it comes out and follows you (same pet as the adoption book, its looks changeable in YOU / PETS).
  • The cage stays full: everyone can buy the same animal.
  • The keeper's counter and the adoption book stay by the door.
