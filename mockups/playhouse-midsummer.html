The Playhouse · West St · every :00 and :30

A Midsummer Night’s Dream Act the First

Mockups of the production: a front curtain, two sets, the lighting for each, the cast and the cue sheet. Pick one letter for each set (or mix them, e.g. “A with C’s fireflies”).

The house and the curtain

A gilt proscenium and a red velvet front curtain are added to the stage you have now (the back drapes, the spotlight and the row of plush seats stay). The curtain is shut between shows, opens on the first line, closes for the scene change and opens again for the curtain call.

Before the show

House lights warm and low, the curtain lit from the front by its footlights, ACT I in gilt on the pelmet.

Curtain up

The house goes dark, the curtain rises in swags and the first set’s light comes up behind it.

Scene I · Athens, the palace of Theseus

The duke and Hippolyta count the days to their wedding by the moon; Egeus drags in Hermia to wed Demetrius, she loves Lysander; the lovers plan to flee to the wood; Helena tells all to Demetrius.

AMoonlit marble court

White columns washed in cold blue, a huge painted crescent moon, two torches burning warm at the sides, haze low on the floor.

cues: blue wash · torch flicker · moon brightens on “another moon”

BGolden hall at dusk

Arches open on a sunset going to violet, red and gold banners, two bronze braziers; it cools to night through the scene.

cues: amber → violet over the scene · brazier glow

CThe court where the wood creeps in

Broken columns under ivy and hanging moss, fireflies drifting, a single moonbeam picks out whoever speaks. The forest of Act II is already showing.

cues: speaker spot · fireflies · green mist

Scene II · Athens, Quince’s house

Peter Quince hands out the parts of “Pyramus and Thisbe”; Bottom wants to play every one; they agree to rehearse in the palace wood by moonlight.

ACandlelit carpenter’s shop

Timber beams, a long workbench, saws and mallets on the wall, shavings on the floor, candle pools in deep amber.

cues: candle flicker · warm pools on the bench

BCottage by moonlight

A dark plaster room, moonlight through a leaded window in a dusty blue shaft, one lantern hanging warm over the table.

cues: window shaft with dust · lantern sway

CPainted flats, played for laughs

Wobbly cut-out flats of a cottage and a sun on a stick, bright footlights, colour gels: a stage that knows it is a stage, like the actors in it.

cues: full footlights · coloured gels · bump on Bottom’s lines

The cast

Thirteen parts, each its own ElevenLabs voice, made once and saved. Bodies are the Walker, dressed per part (the old play’s dresser, re-used).
