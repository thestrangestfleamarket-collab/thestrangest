QUICK BARS

Several bars of your own (favourites) plus ready-made ones for fighting, fishing, mushrooms, birds. Three ways to switch. Click things: these work.

A · THE HANDLE IS THE MENU

Click the handle: a list opens above it. Drag the handle: the bar moves, as now. The bar looks the same as today, only its name sits on the handle. Keys 1 to 0 always play the bar that is showing.

B · TABS ON TOP OF THE BAR

Every bar is a small tab, always in sight; one click (or the mouse wheel over the bar) changes it. ★ are yours, + makes a new one. Takes a little more of the screen.

C · SEVERAL BARS AT ONCE

The handle's list has a tick by each bar: every ticked bar is shown, stacked. The bottom one has keys 1 to 0; the others are clicked (or click a bar's name to bring it to the keys).
THE READY-MADE BARS (same in A, B and C) fill themselves from your pack: what you own shows, what you lack is dim so you know what to get.
FIGHTING: sword, enchanted sword, healing mushroom, cure mushroom, bandage.
FISHING: rod, bait, net, cooler, headlight.
MUSHROOMS: basket, knife, goggles, headlight, map.
BIRDS: bird net, cage, bird seed, binoculars, camera.
TO PICK: 1) A, B or C.  2) Ready-made bars fixed, or can you rearrange them too?  3) Any other ready-made bars (taming, music, riding, stargazing)?
