FIVE RARITIES · THE CARD · PICKING UP

Every thing gets one of five rarities, kept on the thing itself, so every item has one. Finds (mushrooms, fish, butterflies, birds, honey, fossils) roll it by the one rule we have now, stretched to five; shop goods take it from what they're worth. The top one shows only once that skill's trainer ranks you high enough (like super-rare now). Pick the names and colours below; the card and the feed under them follow your pick.

1 · THE FIVE

A · THE USUAL NAMESThe names and colours most games use (Fortnite's): grey, green, blue, purple, gold. Players know them at once. The green one sits close to the menus' green.
B · THE MARKET'S OWN WORDSThe same five colours, named after the game: JUNK, ODD, STRANGE, STRANGER, THE STRANGEST. Its own, and it reads as the place.
C · STARSOne to five stars, one warm colour getting brighter, so it never fights the menus' green. Plain; no words to learn.

THE ODDS OF A FIND, all three: 55 · 25 · 12 · 6 · 2 in a hundred (now 70 · 25 · 5). The top one needs the trainer's EXPERT, the fourth ADEPT; until then the roll gives the one below. Today's COMMON / RARE / SUPER RARE become the 1st, 3rd and 5th.

2 · THE CARD (3B)

ONE CARD EVERYWHEREHover a thing in the bag, the quick bar, a shop, a trade, the vault or the wheel: this card. Its own 3D model turning, the name, its rarity, what it does, worth, weight, uses or charge left, the skill it feeds, and the keys. It takes the place of the line under the bag. On a controller it shows for the thing under the cursor; on a phone a long press.

3 · PICKING UP AND THE FEED (3C)

WALK OVER IT, IT'S YOURSSmall finds go in your bag when you walk over them: mushrooms, flowers, coins, feathers, shells, raw stones. A thing someone dropped, anything big, and anything of the top two rarities still need E (so nothing rare goes by mistake, and nobody hoovers up another player's drop). Each one slides into the feed top right, in its colour, ×count when it repeats, and fades in 4 s. The log keeps its line too. A setting turns walking-over off.
