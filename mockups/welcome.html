THE STRANGEST FLEA MARKET is one city block that everyone shares. The people you see walking around are real players, live.

You're on the subway platform. Go up the stairs to MARKET ST: there are shops to go into, a park to explore, the Hudson to fish and dive in, and your own apartment.

You start with $1,000, a starter pack and a pet. Your skills start at 0 and get better as you use them.
