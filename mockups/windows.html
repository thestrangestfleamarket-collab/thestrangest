ONE WINDOW SYSTEM: FOUR WAYS

All four keep the Codec look (27 Oct) and the 1 Nov HUD (bars top left, minimap, log, quick bar). All four put every menu, shop, talk, activity and editor into the same frame: title bar [ NAME ], a tab row, the body, a footer with the live keys, one X. They differ in where the frame lives and how many can be open. Pick a letter; the smaller pictures under each screen show the same frame holding the menu, a shop and a talk.

WHAT IT IS

  • One frame, one place: a column down the right side.
  • Everything opens into it as a tab: CHARACTER, PACK, MAP, GUIDE, SETTINGS; a shop or a talk adds a tab while it lasts (RADISH), then goes.
  • Only one deck. Never two windows.

HOW IT WORKS

  • ESC opens/closes · TAB / SHIFT+TAB next tab · 1–9 jump to a tab while open.
  • Pad: START opens, LB/RB tabs, stick moves, A picks, B closes. VR: the one panel.
  • Double-clicking a merchant opens the deck on their tab with WARES and TALK as sub-tabs. The pack is one tab away, so buy/sell sees your things.
  • Editors are tabs too (a wider deck when one is on).

WHAT IT FIXES

  • No corner collisions, no stacking, nothing hidden behind anything.
  • One X, one drag (none needed), one place to look.
  • Pad and VR become trivial (one panel).
  • Costs: you can't see the pack and a shop side by side (the shop shows what you carry of its goods instead); the deck covers a third of the picture.

WHAT IT IS

  • The same frame, but four fixed homes: LEFT (you: pack, skills, book), RIGHT (the thing: a shop, inspect, an editor), BOTTOM (the moment: talk, fishing, decorate bar), CENTRE (the menu; dims the rest).
  • Several can be open at once, one per home. A second in the same home replaces it (a counter shows you can Tab back).

HOW IT WORKS

  • Every window kind has a home and never opens anywhere else. No dragging, nothing to arrange.
  • ESC closes the newest; TAB moves focus between open homes; I left, 5 right (inspect), Esc again the menu.
  • Pad: LB/RB jump homes, LT/RT tabs inside one. VR: each home is its own panel at its side.

WHAT IT FIXES

  • Pack and shop side by side; talk under both; inspect while you shop.
  • Every corner collision gone: one window per home, HUD in the gaps.
  • Costs: up to three frames on screen; the picture shrinks to the middle while trading; needs a rule for each of the ~45 windows (which home).

WHAT IT IS

  • Two uses of the one frame. The overlay: Esc fills the screen (the game dimmed and paused for you) with a left nav and a page: CHARACTER, PACK, SKILLS, MAP, GUIDE, SETTINGS, REPORT A BUG, LOG OUT, ADMIN. The bar: everything that happens in the world (talk, a shop, fishing, decorating, the arcade's footer) is the same frame across the bottom third.
  • Nothing floats. Ever.

HOW IT WORKS

  • ESC overlay; I overlay on PACK; M on MAP. In the overlay: ↑↓/1–9 pages, TAB into the page.
  • The bar opens itself when you double-click someone or start an activity; Esc or walking away closes it. Its tabs are the thing's: TALK · WARES · ASKS.
  • Editors are overlay pages (the game not dimmed, so you see what you edit).

WHAT IT FIXES

  • The clearest two states: "I'm in the menu" vs "I'm doing something". Modern-console familiar.
  • Big, readable pages; the pack can show pictures, not rows.
  • Pad/VR: two panels, both simple.
  • Costs: you can't check the pack while a shop is open without leaving the bar (the bar shows what you carry of its goods); the overlay hides the world.

WHAT IT IS

  • Everything is a call: one frame across the bottom third, a portrait on the left (who you talk to, your own face for the menu, the spinning item for inspect, a map tile for the map), the content in the middle, the thing's tabs down the right.
  • Metal Gear codec, all the way. Only one call at a time.

HOW IT WORKS

  • ESC calls yourself (the menu: CHARACTER · PACK · MAP · GUIDE · SETTINGS · BUG · LOG OUT). Double-click someone: calls them (TALK · WARES · ASKS). 5: calls the item.
  • ↑↓ rows, ←→ or TAB tabs, ENTER do, ESC hang up. Pad identical. VR: the one panel, low, where your hands are.
  • Editors: the same call, taller (two thirds), the portrait is the picked thing.

WHAT IT FIXES

  • The strongest single identity; every screen reads the same way at a glance.
  • The world stays visible above the call — you see who you're talking to and where you are.
  • Pad/VR/phone: one bottom panel is the easiest shape there is.
  • Costs: least room per screen (the pack scrolls; the map is small unless the call grows); nothing side by side.
