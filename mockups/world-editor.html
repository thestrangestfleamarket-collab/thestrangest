2 · Whose edits does everyone see? (the big question)

A · LIVE FOR EVERYONE
Every player's change lands in the shared world right away, the way yours do today. Simplest, but one player can wreck the street for everyone. You'd need an undo/log in ADMIN to put things back.
B · MINE NOW, YOURS APPROVES (recommended)
The player sees their own change at once. It goes to your queue (the WORLD EDIT reports that already exist), and everyone else sees it once you APPROVE it.
C · ONLY MINE
Each player's changes stay in their own world and nobody else sees them. Fully safe, but it's more a toy than a shared world.
