Tonight: . Take a seat at the long table, or put a record on the jukebox.

Keyboard and mouse

W A S D walk, Shift run, C zoom
E sit down, stand up
T talk to the one you face
Click: pick up, draw, press a table button, the jukebox
Wheel: choose a card in your hand
Right click: play it; F face down; Q toss; R flip

In VR

Left stick walks, right stick turns
A or X: sit at the nearest free chair, stand
Grip or pinch a card to pick it up
Let go to drop or throw; by your other hand to fan
Trigger points: grab from afar, press buttons; at the jukebox, the next record

The jukebox

Recordings of 1905 to 1925 from the Library of Congress National Jukebox, in the public domain.
