Ride the service elevator to the top floor. The doors open onto one wooden plank, 30 cm wide, sticking out over the city at sunset. Walk to the chalk X at the end and stand on it for ten seconds.

Controls
VR
Walk for real (clear about 2 × 6 m), or push the left stick. Right stick turns. Trigger presses elevator buttons.
Desktop
Drag to look. W A S D or arrow keys to walk. Click the elevator buttons on your right.
Phone
Drag to look. Arrow pad to walk. Tap the elevator buttons.

Convincing heights can cause dizziness, nausea or panic. If you're working on a fear of heights, start at floor 12 and move up. If it gets to be too much, close your eyes and take the headset off.
