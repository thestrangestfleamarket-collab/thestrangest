FOURTH & LONG

Eleven on eleven against the computer. The whole field floats in the air in your room; when your quarterback takes the snap you are standing in the pocket.

  • Call a play: point at a card on the board and pull the right trigger.
  • Before the snap: trigger one of your players, then a spot, an opponent or a new route. A snaps.
  • Quarterback: point at a receiver and pull the trigger (tap a lob, hold a bullet), or hold the right grip, swing and let go. Left stick moves you, B throws it away.
  • With the ball: left stick runs, left trigger sprints, A juke, B spin, X dive, Y hurdle; flick the right stick to juke a side, up for a stiff arm.
  • The board: the coach's pick is ringed; TIMEOUT gives back the seconds a play ran off; MENU has the level, a two-minute drill, practice, weather and the stadium.
  • On defense: A dive tackle, X hit, Y jump for the ball, B switch player.
  • Press the right stick to change view, the left stick to put a stadium round the field. Grips move and size the table.
