Blackmere Hall · the night of the storm · 20 guests

The Guest List

You wrote twenty invitations. All twenty came. The bridge is out, the gates are locked, the telephone is dead, and the storm will hold until six. You are the host. By morning there must be no one left to say what happened here, and until the very end, no one may know it was you.

Your voice
The headset's microphone is open all night. Whatever you say aloud is heard by everyone near enough to hear it, and they answer as themselves. Ask, lie, charm, accuse.
Your hands
Nothing happens by a button. It happens by what your hands do: a thing in your hand, moving fast enough, in the right place, does what it would do. Grips close your hands: on a glass, a knife, a throat, a collar.
Getting about
Left stick walks, right stick turns. Close a hand on a thing to take it; touch a door, drawer, switch or tap and pull the trigger to work it. B lets go of what you hold (a moving hand throws it). Y changes what is in your hand for what is in your pocket. X is the guest list.
A murder mystery from the wrong side. Violence, not gore. Play seated or with clear floor around you.
