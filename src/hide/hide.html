Sixty seconds

Dead End

You are alone in a house in a storm. You have one minute to hide before it comes in at the front door. Wardrobes, beds, cupboards, curtains, the cellar: thirty-seven places will hold a body. It listens, it sees your torch, and it notices what you moved. Leave the television on downstairs and it may go there first.

Headset
Left stick walks. Right stick turns, and pulled down it crouches you. The trigger is your hand: squeeze it on a door, a lid or anything loose to take hold, on a switch to work it. Your torch is in your left hand: squeeze that trigger on nothing to put it out or on. A hand over your mouth holds your breath.
Screen
WASD, Shift to run, C to crouch then lie flat, mouse button to take hold, carry, throw or work a switch, E to open and shut, F for your torch, Space to climb over, Q to hold your breath.
Dark, loud and frightening. Play seated or with clear floor around you.
