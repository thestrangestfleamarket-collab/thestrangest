Sixty seconds

Dead End

You ran into an alley with no way out. You have one minute to hide before it comes in after you. Twenty places will hold a body. It listens, it watches for your breath in its light, and it notices what you moved.

Headset
Left stick walks. Right stick turns, and pulled down it crouches you. Grip carries, throws or climbs. Right trigger opens and shuts. A climbs over. Left trigger, or a hand over your mouth, holds your breath. X changes the difficulty.
Screen
WASD, Shift to run, C to crouch then lie flat, mouse button to carry and throw, E to open and shut, Space to climb over, Q to hold your breath, 1 2 3 for easy, normal, hard.
Dark, loud and frightening. Play seated or with clear floor around you.
