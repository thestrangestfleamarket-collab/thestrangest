Slowfire Duel

Two platforms, slow shots, and your own body as the target. First to win two rounds takes the match.

  • Mouse or finger: move to dodge and to touch floating weapons. Click, Space or FIRE shoots.
  • Headset: dodge for real, reach out to grab weapons, pull the trigger to shoot.
  • Twenty-five weapons turn up, each with limited shots; the readout says what the one in your hand does. Shields block until their power is spent.
  • Q or right-click throws what you hold at your rival, E drops it. In a headset the grip lets go: swing and it flies.
  • A closed room. A disc bounces off two walls and breaks on the third; a hit takes a quarter of your health.
  • Headset: reach behind a shoulder or to a hip, pull the trigger to take a disc, swing and let go to throw. A grip lifts you on the jetpack, both grips lift faster; lean to slide sideways.
  • Screen: click throws at the mark, so aim at a wall to bank it. Space lifts, Shift lifts faster, A and D slide.
  • A disc in your hand knocks theirs away. An empty hand can catch one: in a headset pull the trigger as it reaches your hand, on a screen press F or right-click as it arrives. Mistime it and it counts as a hit.
  • Each disc comes back three seconds after you take it. The jetpack holds ten seconds and refills on the ground.
