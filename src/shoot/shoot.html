VR controls
Grip (hold)Grab a weapon from its holster: pistol on right hip, rifle over right shoulder, shotgun over left shoulder, grenades on chest. Let go and it snaps back to its holster.
TriggerFire. Holding a grenade: arm it (3 s fuse), then release grip to throw.
Second handGrip the fore-end to steady rifle or shotgun (much less recoil). Slide it back and forward to pump the shotgun.
A / XDrop magazine. Grab a fresh one at your belt with the free hand and push it into the magwell. Shotgun: bring shells from belt to the loading port.
B / YRelease a locked bolt after reloading — or grip the slide / charging handle and pull back. Otherwise toggles the flashlight.
Left stickMove (click to sprint). Right stick: turn. Physically crouch and lean to use cover.
