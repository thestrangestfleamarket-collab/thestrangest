COLD BORE

Five contracts for one rifle · VR or screen
In the headset
GripTake the rifle by its pistol grip. It waits on the sandbags in front of you. Let go on the bipod and it stays there.
BipodSet the bipod's feet on the sandbags: the rifle steadies and pivots there. Bring your eye to the scope, about a hand's width behind it.
TriggerFire. On the other hand: hold your breath (a few seconds of calm). Pointing at the board: press its buttons.
BoltAfter each shot grip the bolt knob with a free hand, pull it back and push it home. Or press A / X on the rifle hand.
MagazineB / Y on the rifle hand drops it. Grip a fresh one from the tins on the sandbags and push it into the well, then work the bolt.
SticksRifle hand: up and down turns the elevation turret, left and right the windage, a tenth of a mil a click. Other hand: zoom, 5x to 25x.
A / X, other handLaser rangefinder: the range shows in the scope and on the card by it, with the elevation to dial.
On a screen
MouseAim. Right button (or Q) brings up the scope, left fires, wheel zooms.
W S / A DElevation / windage turrets (the arrows too)
E · Shift · RRangefinder · hold breath · reload
How a shot works
The round drops and drifts as a real one does. Range the target, dial the UP from the card on the rifle, then read the wind: the card gives the hold for each metre a second of crosswind, and the reticle's marks are one mil apart. Far targets take over a second to reach: lead a walking man. Your spotter calls each miss in mils.
