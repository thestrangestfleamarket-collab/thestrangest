SPACE FLEET

A fleet battle that floats in your own room, or in open space. For a Meta Quest headset, in its browser.

  • Your ships fly and fight on their own. You give the orders. A guide in the headset shows you how, one step at a time.
  • Trigger on a ship and drag it where it should go, or onto a target.
  • Thumbsticks zoom, turn and fly; click one to pause. Grips drag the field.
  • The console floating at your left is the shipyard: point either controller at a ship on it and pull the trigger. Hold the relays for more income. Destroy their mothership.
