SENSOR TAG

A laser duel in your own room. Both players wear a Quest 3; each of you has a sensor panel on your chest and a pistol in your right hand.

  • 10 lives each. One shot a second, a straight beam.
  • Only a beam that reaches the chest sensor takes a life.
  • Turn away, duck, step aside, or put a hand over your sensor: no hit.
  • Out of lives: you lose.
