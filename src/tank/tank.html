Rolling out…

Three tanks · four missions · alone or up to four in the lobby

Hull
Down

You are alone in the turret. Enemy armour is coming over the far ridges. Drive up behind a crest until only your turret shows, lase the range, lay the gun through the sight, and fire first. Two more tanks of your platoon hold the line with you.

Settings and comfort

Controls
VR
Sit down. Left stick drives, right stick lays the gun. Right hand: trigger fires, grip lases the range, A is the hatch, B zooms the sight, stick click re-centres your seat. Left hand: trigger lases, grip fires smoke, X changes the round, Y is the thermal sight, stick click is the extinguisher. Stick click also sets your eye level to the seat. Hold the left trigger, then the right, for three seconds to send the owners a picture of what you see. Head out, your kit hangs on you: pistol on your right hip, magazines at your belt, grenades on your chest, mines on your left hip. Reach, squeeze the grip to take one, and let go to throw a grenade or drop a mine; the trigger fires the pistol, and a magazine brought to it reloads it. Inside, hold the left trigger for the coaxial gun.
Desktop
Click to take the mouse: it lays the gun. W A S D drive. Left click or Space fires. Right click or F is the gunsight, Z or the wheel its zoom (four steps), T thermal. P sends the owners a picture of what you see. R lases the range. 1 and 2 pick SABOT or HEAT. G fires smoke. X is the extinguisher. H is the hatch. Head out you carry a pistol (3), grenades (4) and mines for the ground (5): click uses what is in your hand. Inside, hold C for the coaxial gun against men on foot. Stop by the supply truck on the road to rearm and repair.
Phone
Drag to lay the gun. Arrow pad drives. Fire, Sight and Hatch are on the right.

In a headset the turret turns the world around you. If it gets uncomfortable, stop the turret, close your eyes and take the headset off.
