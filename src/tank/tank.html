Rolling out…

Three tanks · four waves · one against one when someone joins

Hull
Down

You are alone in the turret. Enemy armour is coming over the far ridges. Drive up behind a crest until only your turret shows, lase the range, lay the gun through the sight, and fire first. Two more tanks of your platoon hold the line with you.

Controls
VR
Sit down. Left stick drives, right stick lays the gun. Right hand: trigger fires, grip lases the range, A is the hatch, B zooms the sight, stick click re-centres your seat. Left hand: trigger lases, grip fires smoke, X changes the round, Y is the thermal sight, stick click is the extinguisher. Head out, hold the left grip for the binoculars and bring them to your eyes; left trigger then brings the gun on to what you see. Head out, the right grip changes what your right hand holds (pistol, grenade, mine) and the trigger uses it; inside, hold the left trigger for the coaxial gun.
Desktop
Click to take the mouse: it lays the gun. W A S D drive. Left click or Space fires. Right click or F is the gunsight, Z its zoom, T thermal. R lases the range. 1 and 2 pick SABOT or HEAT. G fires smoke. X is the extinguisher. H is the hatch. Head out, B is the binoculars: look, then click to bring the gun on to what you see. Head out you also carry a pistol (3), grenades (4) and mines for the ground (5): click uses what is in your hand. Inside, hold C for the coaxial gun against men on foot. Stop by the supply truck on the road to rearm and repair.
Phone
Drag to lay the gun. Arrow pad drives. Fire, Sight and Hatch are on the right.

In a headset the turret turns the world around you. If it gets uncomfortable, stop the turret, close your eyes and take the headset off.
