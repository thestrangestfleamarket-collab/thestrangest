SWIVEL

You are the gunner in the ball turret on a gunship's back. They come from every side. Sit in a chair that spins.

  • Headset: point a controller and hold its trigger to fire; a grip sends a homing missile at the boxed ship. No spinning chair? A thumbstick turns you.
  • Screen: the mouse aims, hold the button to fire, right click or F for a missile. A and D turn too.
  • Aim at the yellow ring ahead of a ship, not the ship. Red arrows point at what you are not looking at.
  • The guns overheat. The shield comes back when you stop being hit; the hull does not.
