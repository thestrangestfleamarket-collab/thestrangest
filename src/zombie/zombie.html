New York, 1985 · 31st floor · Night 1 of 5

Eyes Down

It's after eleven. Lock up, eat, watch the news, brush your teeth, go to bed. There are things in your apartment, black and wet with pale eyes, standing where your chores make you look. Live your evening as if they weren't there. If you look one in the eye, it knows you can see it.

Headset
Left stick walks, right stick turns. Point and pull the right trigger to use things. Grip picks things up. Left trigger, or both hands over your eyes, shuts them. B stands you up. Your watch has the time and tonight's list.
Screen
WASD and the mouse. E uses what you look at. Hold the mouse button to carry. Hold Space to shut your eyes. Hold Tab for tonight's list.
Hunted
The lights die. You have four seconds: shut yourself in the bathroom with your eyes closed, or get under the covers. Not the same place twice.
Frightening. Play seated or with clear floor around you.
